# `onboarding-proof`

What this proof measures and re-verifies. The [proof registry](README.md) carries its area, prerequisites,
platforms, flags and neighbours; the script is [`apps/desktop/scripts/onboarding-proof.mjs`](../../apps/desktop/scripts/onboarding-proof.mjs).

The first-launch setup of [`PD-WORKSPACE-010`](../product/directives/pd-workspace-010.md#pd-workspace-010), through the driver and with no provider: a fresh profile with no project opens on the setup at its project step, the shell inert beneath it, REQ-SHELL-011's deferred chooser unscheduled and REQ-WORKSPACE-152 (3)'s notice held back; the step's own press opens the chooser, the window is replaced and the replacement opens on the model step; a relaunch resumes there; Skip setup clears it, stores `done` and lets the notice be published; a third launch shows none. Its last launch is the seam every other proof names (`ROTARIS_CAPTURE_ONBOARDING=off`): no setup, the deferred chooser offered. The sign-in, the slots, the mode and the first prompt are measured where they are owned (`slice-proof`, `config-proof`, `composer-proof`).
