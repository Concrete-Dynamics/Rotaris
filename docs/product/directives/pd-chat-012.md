### PD-CHAT-012 — each transcript surface owns one polite live region that speaks a tool row on arrival and every row on completion, never a stream

Each transcript surface — the main window's transcript and the "Agent transcript" card of a persona window (REQ-SHELL-075) — MUST own exactly one polite live region, mounted with its window, present and empty before any row arrives, separate from the row list and holding no interactive control. Through it: a tool row MUST be announced on arrival by its header's accessible name as REQ-CHAT-178 orders it, and once more on completion with the outcome REQ-CHAT-060 appends; every other row MUST be announced exactly once, when it completes, by its accessible name and its own text; a thinking row's reasoning content MUST NOT be announced; a streamed delta, a row's text while it is still changing, and the lines of an embedded terminal preview MUST NOT be announced while they change; a row outside the scope toggle's current selection (REQ-CHAT-069) MUST NOT be announced; and the region of a window that is not the active window MUST NOT announce. The row list itself MUST NOT be a live region. No announcement under this directive is assertive. Every announced text is text the record already fixes — the row's accessible name, REQ-CHAT-060's outcome, the row's own content — and no wording is added for an announcement. The transcript's empty state stays ordinary content of its region (REQ-NOTIFY-006), not an announcement.

**Why, and what it costs.** The record orders what a tool row's name says (REQ-CHAT-178) and what its outcome appends (REQ-CHAT-060) but names no surface that speaks a streamed row, and the build filled the gap two ways at once: the main transcript, its toolbar included, and the persona window's row list are each a polite `log`, so a streaming row re-announces on every delta and the embedded preview's lines are read as they change, which is the token-by-token speech guide 09 lists as the anti-pattern a reader talks over itself with. Raised as [OQ-507](../../archive/architecture/open-questions/70-spikes-and-later-questions-37.md#oq-507). One region per surface, speaking a row when it completes, bounds speech to events the operator can act on; the gate on the active window keeps two windows showing the same rows (REQ-SHELL-075) from speaking twice. The cost is that a long reply is heard only once it is finished, that an operator working in another window hears nothing from this one until they return to it, and that the two list-wide live regions are removed; the gain is that a screen-reader operator hears each row once, whole, in REQ-CHAT-178's order, and that no control is read as text. No requirement is displaced: REQ-CHAT-178, REQ-CHAT-069 and REQ-SHELL-075 govern in full and the directive adds the speaker beside them. How a reader renders the region is not settled here — NVDA and VoiceOver differ and no proof drives one — so a `debug-ui`, `review-ui` or autopsy finding of a flood, a duplicate or an unspoken completion reopens the question; an announcement-frequency preference would be a new Settings control and is a request of the owner's.

> **Extends:** REQ-CHAT-178, REQ-CHAT-069, REQ-SHELL-075

*Raised as [OQ-507](../../archive/architecture/open-questions/70-spikes-and-later-questions-37.md#oq-507) by [R-093](../requests.md#r-093), 2026-11-03, and ruled the same day by the orchestrator's consortium under the owner's delegation ([R-072](../requests.md#r-072)); minted because a live region that speaks a transcript row once, on completion, is behaviour beside REQ-CHAT-178 that no requirement states and the build had filled two ways.*
