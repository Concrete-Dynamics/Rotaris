# References — the design research corpus

**Review date:** 2026-11-01

Every source the fourteen guides cite, deduplicated and ordered by author or organisation. A citation supports the claim it is attached to in the guide, not an assertion that the source endorses this product's reading of it. Links point at primary documentation or the original publication, never a summary; a page that may change carries the date it was retrieved.

*The catalogue is written from the guides once they are filled. Until then it holds the entry shape only.*
