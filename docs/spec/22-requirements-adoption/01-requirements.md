## Requirements

### The surface

- **REQ-ADOPT-001** — The requirements board SHALL carry an **adoption strip** beneath its header that states the adoption state of the open workspace in one sentence — *not adopted*, *adopted with no change since*, or *adopted with changes since* — and offers the adoption controls. The strip is not part of the header, and none of its controls SHALL be presented as the primary action: REQ-TRACE-491's one primary control stays the plain re-evaluation control, and every adoption control is a secondary control of the ordinary height.
- **REQ-ADOPT-002** — The strip SHALL offer exactly three adoption controls: **Run adoption** (an initial run, or a re-run where the workspace is already adopted), **Record current state** (shown only where the workspace is adopted), and a **Write layout file** choice that applies to the next run. Each control SHALL describe what it does in wording consistent with it: Run adoption consults a model and writes the adoption record; Record current state consults no model and writes the adoption record; the layout choice writes `rotaris-traceability.json` at the repository root. Beside them the strip MAY offer one **disclosure** control that shows and hides the lists of REQ-ADOPT-023 and the undecided items of REQ-ADOPT-010; it writes nothing and consults nothing. The state sentence SHALL name the new requirements itself, at most five and the rest counted, so that the headline case needs no disclosure.
- **REQ-ADOPT-003** — An adoption run or a recording SHALL start only when the user presses its control. No refresh, re-evaluation, timer, window focus, channel-10 change or opening of the board SHALL start one, and no other surface SHALL start one on the user's behalf.
- **REQ-ADOPT-004** — While a run or a recording is in flight the strip SHALL show that it is, SHALL disable its own controls, and SHALL leave every other board control as it was. Its outcome — the adopted store, the identifier family and the counts written, or the refusal with its reason — SHALL replace the in-flight line when it arrives.

### The detection pass

- **REQ-ADOPT-005** — The detection pass SHALL be **read-only** and SHALL consult no model. It walks the workspace from its root, never follows a symbolic link out of it, and SHALL skip the version-control directory, the workspace scope directory (`.rotaris`), every hidden directory, and the components `node_modules`, `dist`, `build`, `out`, `target`, `vendor`, `coverage` and `fixtures`.
- **REQ-ADOPT-006** — The pass SHALL be **bounded**: at most 20,000 files enumerated, at most 512 KiB read from any one file and at most 64 MiB read in all. Reaching a bound is not an error: the result SHALL say which bound was reached, and SHALL name the consequence among what it could not decide (REQ-ADOPT-010).
- **REQ-ADOPT-007** — The pass SHALL report every **candidate requirements store** it found, each with the directory, the identifier prefix, the definition shape, the number of documents and of distinct identifiers it defines, and the reasons it was taken as a candidate. A definition shape is one of: an identifier block in frontmatter (REQ-TRACE-008), a bold identifier opening a bullet, a heading opening with an identifier, a bold identifier opening a line, and a table row whose first cell is an identifier. An identifier merely mentioned in running text SHALL NOT make a candidate.
- **REQ-ADOPT-008** — For each candidate the pass SHALL state the **numbering** its identifiers follow: `contiguous` where every number from the lowest to the highest is defined, `sparse` with the count of gaps otherwise, `dotted` where any identifier carries a dotted number (`FR-1.2`), and `zero-padded` where any number carries a leading zero (`REQ-001`). A dotted or zero-padded family, and a family whose prefix holds a hyphen (`REQ-AUTH-1`), cannot be written as REQ-TRACE-078's `<PREFIX>-<digits>` under REQ-TRACE-038's `id_prefix`; each SHALL still be reported as a candidate, SHALL be named as undecidable, and SHALL NOT be adopted.
- **REQ-ADOPT-009** — The pass SHALL report the **implementation roots** and **test roots** it found — the top-level directories holding source files, and the directories and file-name patterns holding tests — and the **annotation vocabulary** the code uses: for each source extension, the comment forms in which an identifier of a candidate's family occurs, with their counts and at most five sample lines, each cut to 160 characters.
- **REQ-ADOPT-010** — The pass SHALL report what it could **not decide**, each item by name and with its reason: no candidate store; several candidate stores; several identifier families; dotted numbering; zero-padded numbering; a namespaced prefix; a candidate whose documents mix definition shapes; tests that sit beside the code they test; no annotation of any candidate family in source; a bound reached. It SHALL NOT resolve any of them by guessing.

### The adapter

- **REQ-ADOPT-011** — An adoption run SHALL compose an **adapter** from the detection result and one **interpretation**: the layout document (the keys of REQ-TRACE-038), its annotation conventions (REQ-TRACE-073), and the **extraction** that maps the project's identifier form to the record form — the identifier-block extraction of REQ-TRACE-008 where the candidate's shape is frontmatter, otherwise a definition pattern with exactly one capture group that yields the identifier.
- **REQ-ADOPT-012** — The interpretation SHALL be one bounded request to the model the `requirements-engineer` role resolves to (REQ-CONFIG-098), carrying the detection result and nothing read from the workspace beyond the result's own sample lines. It SHALL be asked to choose one candidate by its identifier, the identifier prefix, the definition pattern, the implementation and test roots, and the annotation conventions. No configured model for the role is a refusal naming the role.
- **REQ-ADOPT-013** — The interpretation is **untrusted input**. Before anything is written or executed, the answer SHALL be read as one JSON object holding only the keys of the interpretation (Part 02), and every field SHALL be validated: the candidate SHALL be one the detection reported; the prefix SHALL satisfy REQ-TRACE-038's `id_prefix` rule; every path SHALL satisfy REQ-TRACE-043 and name an existing directory inside the root; every convention SHALL satisfy REQ-TRACE-073; the definition pattern SHALL compile, hold exactly one capture group and be at most 300 characters; and the layout document composed from them SHALL parse under REQ-TRACE-045 and REQ-TRACE-046.
- **REQ-ADOPT-014** — The store directory of the composed layout SHALL be the chosen candidate's directory as the **detection** reported it, never a path the interpretation names. The interpretation SHALL NOT be able to name a file to write, and no path it supplies SHALL resolve, after symbolic links, outside the workspace root.
- **REQ-ADOPT-015** — An answer that is empty, carries no JSON object, carries an unknown key, or fails any validation SHALL be a **refusal** naming the field and the reason. A refusal writes nothing of the assistant's, leaves the board and its generation unchanged, and is reported on the strip; it SHALL NOT raise out of the workspace service. The configuration load that resolves the role's model keeps the workspace scope directory ignored (REQ-CONFIG-013) as every load does; that maintenance is the configuration's and not the assistant's.

### Executing and recording

- **REQ-ADOPT-016** — A validated adapter SHALL be **executed** before anything is written: its extraction runs over the store directory and yields, for each document, the content hash of REQ-TRACE-036 and the identifiers it defines, and for each identifier, the canonical hash of REQ-TRACE-037 over its definition text. An execution that yields no identifier of the chosen prefix SHALL be a refusal.
- **REQ-ADOPT-017** — A definition pattern SHALL be applied one line at a time, to lines of at most 2,000 characters; a longer line is passed over and counted. A requirement's definition text runs from its defining line to the line before the next definition or the next heading.
- **REQ-ADOPT-018** — On a successful execution the run SHALL write the **adoption record** (Part 02) to `.rotaris/requirements-adoption.json` in the workspace, replacing any earlier record whole, and SHALL write nothing else unless the layout choice of REQ-ADOPT-002 was set.
- **REQ-ADOPT-019** — With the layout choice set, the run SHALL also write the composed layout document to `rotaris-traceability.json` at the repository root (REQ-TRACE-040) **only where no such file exists**. An existing file SHALL NOT be overwritten: the run reports that it was kept, and the record is written all the same.
- **REQ-ADOPT-020** — **Record current state** SHALL execute the adapter the current record holds, without a model, and write a new record from its result, so that the changes the strip reports are taken as the new baseline. Where no record exists it SHALL be refused.
- **REQ-ADOPT-021** — Every write of the record SHALL replace the file atomically, so that a reader sees the previous record or the new one and never a part of either.

### The comparison on opening

- **REQ-ADOPT-022** — Opening the board, and every re-reading of the adoption state, SHALL **compare** the store as it now stands against the recorded set by executing the recorded adapter. This is a **read**: it SHALL write no file in the workspace or its scope directory, SHALL consult no model (REQ-TRACE-341), and SHALL NOT move the board's generation.
- **REQ-ADOPT-023** — The comparison SHALL report, by identifier and in identifier order, the requirements that are **new** (extracted now and absent from the record), **changed** (present in both with a different canonical hash) and **removed** (in the record and no longer extracted), and the documents added, changed and removed by content hash. New requirements SHALL be reported first.
- **REQ-ADOPT-024** — Where no record exists the comparison SHALL report the workspace as not adopted and SHALL carry the detection result, so the strip can say what an adoption run would start from.
- **REQ-ADOPT-025** — A record that cannot be read, is not valid JSON, carries an unknown version, or whose adapter no longer validates under REQ-ADOPT-013 SHALL be reported as an unreadable record with its reason, and treated as no record for the strip's controls. It SHALL NOT be repaired, rewritten or removed by the comparison.

### Progress and refusal

- **REQ-ADOPT-026** — A run SHALL report its progress through the board's progress protocol (REQ-TRACE-210 to REQ-TRACE-216) and its existing phase words: `reading` for the detection pass and the store extraction, `adopting` for the interpretation, `recording` for the writes.
- **REQ-ADOPT-027** — The interpretation SHALL be bounded at 180 seconds. A model that answers only with reasoning and no content, or exceeds the bound, SHALL be a refusal saying so.
- **REQ-ADOPT-028** — The adoption commands SHALL travel inside the board's command family (REQ-TRACE-378): the renderer names the command, main replaces the workspace with the one it has open and stamps the actor (REQ-CONFIG-198), and the workspace service performs every read and write. A renderer SHALL NOT send an actor and SHALL NOT name a path.
- **REQ-ADOPT-029** — A run SHALL NOT alter a requirement document, the generated artifact, a baseline, the retired-identifier log or the delivery store. What it writes is the record of REQ-ADOPT-018 and, by choice, the layout file of REQ-ADOPT-019, and nothing else; the ignore entries REQ-CONFIG-013 keeps are written by the configuration load that resolves the role's model, as on every load, and not by the assistant.
- **REQ-ADOPT-030** — Two runs in one workspace SHALL NOT overlap: a run or recording requested while one is in flight SHALL be refused with the reason, and the one in flight continues.
