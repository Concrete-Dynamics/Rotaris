# `continuation-proof`

What this proof measures and re-verifies. The [proof registry](README.md) carries its area, prerequisites,
platforms, flags and neighbours; the script is [`apps/desktop/scripts/continuation-proof.mjs`](../../apps/desktop/scripts/continuation-proof.mjs).

PD-ORCH-004 revisions 3 and 4 in the built app (since fin42): a child cut off at a 3 s per-agent timeout while making progress leaves a continuation offer that a later root takes up — (`later-iteration`) iteration 2's root, the queued prompt's, told the offer in its first message, its continuation cut off too and offered none of its own (continued once, never chained); (`later-run`) a new run's root, after a relaunch that opens the session from the Sessions browser with the limit raised to 60 s (through Settings › Runtime › Per-agent timeout and Save, whose field description states the stored 3 s until Save and 60 s after it, REQ-CONFIG-446/419, since 2026-10-15), told it in the continuation handoff, its continuation `succeeded`. Each asserts from the store the cut-off child's report ("Child timed out after 3s", the cut-off statement, the offer), the continuation's `continuation_of` and the later root that delegated it, that the delegating root's own first message listed no offer, and that no offer is left open. The scripted root takes an offer with `continue:<persona>:<name>` (`offeredTaskId`). `--only=<scenario>[,…]`; every scenario runs to its end and failures are listed in `results.json`
