### PD-SHELL-015 — six primary views: Mission is not one

1. **The views.** The main window MUST host exactly six primary views in this fixed order: Overview, Workspace, Requirements, Git, Library, Settings. Exactly one is visible at a time. Their identifiers are `overview`, `workspace`, `requirements`, `git`, `library`, `settings`. The six keep the order REQ-SHELL-023 gave them; Requirements is the third entry, ordered after Workspace and before Git.
2. **Mission is not a primary view.** No rail button, shortcut, command-palette entry, slash-catalogue entry or navigation action names it. The identifier `mission` is retired and MUST NOT be reassigned; a request to switch to it is REQ-SHELL-025's silent no-op. What the view held is placed by [`PD-MONITOR-009`](pd-monitor-009.md#pd-monitor-009), and the command palette MUST find the Overview by the everyday words that found Mission (REQ-SHELL-053).
3. **The rail and the shortcuts.** The rail MUST show one button per view in clause 1's order, labelled "Overview", "Workspace", "Requirements", "Git", "Library", "Settings"; everything else REQ-SHELL-027 to REQ-SHELL-032 state about a rail button stands as written. Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+6 MUST open the six views in that order, under the labels "Open Overview", "Open Workspace", "Open Requirements", "Open Git", "Open Library", "Open Settings". Ctrl+7 MUST be bound to nothing. Every other row of REQ-SHELL-049's table stands as written, Ctrl+, among them.
4. **Where a requirement counts or lists the views.** A requirement that speaks of "the seven primary views", of "the seven views", or of the views of REQ-SHELL-023 is read as the six of clause 1, and one that lists them is read without Mission: REQ-SHELL-005 (each of the six opens populated in demo mode), REQ-SHELL-022 (the minimum-size conditions hold on each of the six), REQ-SHELL-115 (a banner or empty-state action may navigate to any of the six), REQ-SHELL-132 (the theme-switch guarantees cover the six), REQ-SHELL-144 (the contrast and accessible-name sweep covers the six and every tab page inside them), REQ-SHELL-164 (that sweep's scope), REQ-SHELL-069 (the per-view key strings are the six lower-cased names; no `mission` key is read or written), REQ-NOTIFY-010 (each of the six carries its onboarding, primary action, progress presentation and disabled reasons), REQ-ORCH-155 (the surfaces it lists are reachable within the six), REQ-STARTUP-134 (one per-view entry for each of the six), REQ-WORKSPACE-015 (the window hosts exactly the six, in clause 1's order) and REQ-LIBRARY-102 (the Library is one of six top-level views in clause 1's order, reached by Ctrl+5, the key clause 3 assigns it, as that requirement's own last sentence provides). Each of those requirements governs in full outside that count, that list and that key.
5. **What does not move.** The view shown at startup is Overview, as REQ-SHELL-026 states, and Overview and Workspace keep Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+2.

**Why, and what it costs.** Decision D1 of [the information-architecture map](../../design/information-architecture.md), which holds the measurements, the alternatives not taken and the critique. On the built application at `880a08a9` the Mission view held nothing of its own. Its delegation tree is the tree the Overview and the Workspace already draw (REQ-MONITOR-038 names the three surfaces); its activity table and its two run controls are read beside a session's figures, which stand one navigation away on the Overview, whose own agents card carried a link whose only purpose was to leave for Mission; and its third card was a second editor for two settings (`pnpm mission-proof`, launch `painted`; `pnpm overview-proof`, launch `run`; both at 1440×900). A primary view is the most expensive place this product has: a rail entry, a shortcut, a palette entry, a row in every sweep. One that holds a duplicate, a table and a mirror does not earn it.

What a user can now do that they could not: read the agents of a run and the run's figures on one screen, with no navigation between them (`PD-MONITOR-009` states the placement and its own price). What it costs: the rail entry, Ctrl+3 and the palette's "Open Mission" are removed, their capability standing on the Overview; Requirements, Git, Library and Settings each move down one digit (Ctrl+4…7 become Ctrl+3…6), which the product can afford once, before its first release; and an operator who knew Mission by name finds no such entry, only the Overview answering to "mission", "agents", "delegation", "tree" and "team" in the palette.

**Proposed, and refused by this round's own critique.** The first draft of this directive also put Workspace first in the rail and opened the window on it. It is not decided, for three reasons the critique gave and the map records: the ruling of [OQ-506](../../archive/architecture/open-questions/70-spikes-and-later-questions-37.md#oq-506) rests on the returning operator landing on the Overview's sessions card by REQ-SHELL-026; the Overview's header already starts work from every state ("New session"), so "the first screen cannot start work" overstated the finding; and no usage data says whether a launch more often starts new work or resumes parked work, which is the ground on which the same map refuses to change the Library's default tab. Clause 5 states the result. Which view a relaunch shows is raised, not ruled, as [OQ-548](../../architecture/open-questions/71-spikes-and-later-questions-38.md#oq-548).

> **Narrows:** REQ-SHELL-023, REQ-SHELL-027, REQ-SHELL-049, REQ-SHELL-069, REQ-SHELL-005, REQ-SHELL-022, REQ-SHELL-115, REQ-SHELL-132, REQ-SHELL-144, REQ-SHELL-164, REQ-NOTIFY-010, REQ-ORCH-155, REQ-STARTUP-134, REQ-WORKSPACE-015, REQ-LIBRARY-102

Its case on each requirement is the count, the list, the order and the keys of the primary views, stated exhaustively in clauses 1 to 4, and is disjoint from the cases of the directives that already narrow three of them: [`PD-SHELL-004`](pd-shell-004.md#pd-shell-004) on REQ-SHELL-005 (the network silence is the demo window's), [`PD-SHELL-013`](pd-shell-013.md#pd-shell-013) on REQ-SHELL-022 (the opening size on a small work area) and [`PD-SHELL-005`](pd-shell-005.md#pd-shell-005) on REQ-SHELL-115 (the update notice's action). It leaves the extensions of REQ-SHELL-022 ([`PD-SHELL-003`](pd-shell-003.md#pd-shell-003), [`PD-SHELL-014`](pd-shell-014.md#pd-shell-014)) and of REQ-SHELL-049 ([`PD-SHELL-011`](pd-shell-011.md#pd-shell-011)) as they are.
