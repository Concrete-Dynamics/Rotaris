# References — the design research corpus

**Review date:** 2026-11-02

Every source the fourteen guides cite, deduplicated and ordered by author or organisation. A citation supports the claim it is attached to in the guide, not an assertion that the source endorses this product's reading of it.

Links point at primary documentation or the original publication, never a summary; a page that may change carries the date it was retrieved.

## Parts

The catalogue holds 585 sources, numbered once across 8 parts in alphabetical order. A page reached by two addresses (`http` and `https`, a trailing slash, a fragment, an abstract and its PDF, a mirror of the same paper) is one entry. The entries under "Rotaris record" are this repository's own documents, cited as the product's experience rather than as research.
