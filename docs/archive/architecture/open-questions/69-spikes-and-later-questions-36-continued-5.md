**OQ-468 — Do the workspace-boundary sentences the user reads overclaim where OS confinement is inactive?** *(REQ-WORKSPACE-022, REQ-TOOLS-131, REQ-CONFIG-327, REQ-TOOLS-129, ADR-0022, IC-8. Raised 2026-11-01 by the ruling of OQ-464, which asked for it and did not rule it.)*

**What is open.** OQ-464 ruled the boundary per tool: the fence binds every first-party tool that takes a path, the gate's key scoping binds a tool call carrying a path key, and the terminal and MCP are bounded by OS confinement where it exists and by nothing on native Windows (IC-8). Three sentences the user reads say more. REQ-WORKSPACE-022 and REQ-TOOLS-131 state, verbatim, "File access: agents may read and write inside this workspace folder only." REQ-CONFIG-327 states that "any call reaching a path outside the workspace continues to require approval". Where confinement is inactive a shell command reaches any path the operating system allows, and the sentences promise what the mechanism does not hold.

**What the build proceeds under.** The sentences stay as the requirements state them; a change to those strings needs a directive, which OQ-464's ruling did not mint. The hardening plan hardens the fence (bundle `fence-hardening`) and adds OQ-464's invariant test, and changes no user-facing wording.

**What decides it.** A ruling on whether the wording is narrowed where confinement is inactive (a directive over the three requirements, stating the terminal's and MCP's boundary as it is), or left as the target's own words with the gap recorded in `docs/architecture/hardening.md`.

**Ruled 2026-11-04 under the owner's delegation (R-072).** Advocate A (the user's lens) recommended three directives, one per requirement, keeping the short forms and the `outside-workspace!` explanation, narrowing the `workspace-scoped` explanation to the file tools and adding a second sentence keyed to the focused session's confinement result, and rewriting the autonomous prompt without its residual-guard clause; Advocate B (the system's lens) recommended one directive over the three requirements, two fixed strings scoped by tool and not by confinement status, the confinement fact left to the surfaces that own it, and the autonomous prompt restated as REQ-TOOLS-044 fixes it. Ruled: the wording is narrowed by one directive, and the strings stay fixed. The three sentences overclaim on the record OQ-464 settled: REQ-TOOLS-129 fences file access by resolved real path, REQ-TOOLS-130 limits the opt-out to "the file tools and the permission gate's workspace-root scoping", REQ-TOOLS-018 scopes by the seven path keys and never parses `command`, and the terminal and MCP are bounded by ADR-0022's confinement where it exists and by nothing on native Windows (IC-8), which REQ-SANDBOX-130 makes the product say plainly; so "agents may read and write inside this workspace folder only" (REQ-WORKSPACE-022, REQ-TOOLS-131) is true of the file tools and false of a shell command, and REQ-CONFIG-327's "any call reaching a path outside the workspace continues to require approval" is false even for the file tools, since REQ-TOOLS-044 is "exclusive and normative" that such a path "never raises an approval prompt" under `autonomous` and is refused by the fence or allowed by the opt-out. A sentence conditioned on the focused session's confinement result is refused: REQ-SHELL-041 gives each flag one full-sentence explanation whose words match the Settings control that produces it, REQ-WORKSPACE-022 fixes the pair "because they are the pair a reader compares", REQ-TOOLS-184 has the strip mirror the settings pages, REQ-SANDBOX-121 already reports the confinement result in the item right after the flag (PD-SHELL-008) and REQ-SANDBOX-130 names the two surfaces that carry the no-confinement disclosure, and REQ-CONFIG-302(b) makes `workspace-write` a bound on writes and not reads, so "confined" would overclaim for reads where confinement is on and say nothing with no session focused. The `outside-workspace!` explanation is true and keeps its words; only the `workspace-scoped` explanation, REQ-TOOLS-131's reading of it and REQ-CONFIG-327's residual guard depart. PD-WORKSPACE-007 (R-071, the owner's ruling of OQ-191) is not reversed and not revised: OQ-191 disputed five other statements and the owner ruled only those; its sentence that "the file-access pair's fixed sentences" still govern is a scoping sentence that left the pair outside the dispute and ruled nothing on the pair's truth, so for the `workspace-scoped` sentence it yields to this ruling and the `outside-workspace!` sentence it keeps. The departure mints [`PD-WORKSPACE-009`](../../../product/directives/pd-workspace-009.md#pd-workspace-009), narrowing REQ-WORKSPACE-022, REQ-TOOLS-131 and REQ-CONFIG-327 per identifier: one fixed `workspace-scoped` explanation stating the boundary per tool, the autonomous prompt saying what REQ-TOOLS-044 keeps, and no runtime change — the terminal and MCP stay bounded only by confinement where it is available, as OQ-464 ruled and `docs/architecture/hardening/02-the-findings.md` records. The build proceeds under the directive: `WORKSPACE_SCOPED_EXPLANATION` in `packages/ui/src/composer.ts` and its assertion in `packages/ui/src/composer.test.ts` take the new string; the autonomous `Confirm` body in `packages/ui/src/settings/ConfigSettings.tsx`, which already omits REQ-CONFIG-327's clause with no directive (an unrecorded departure, written to `docs/defects/` and moved to `fixed/` by the same landing), takes the directive's three statements and cites it, pinned by a test; the three requirements gain back-pointers in the same commit, PD-WORKSPACE-007 gains a dated note after "the file-access pair's fixed sentences" saying the `workspace-scoped` sentence is narrowed by PD-WORKSPACE-009 (OQ-468) and that it ruled nothing on the pair, the hardening row for OQ-464 points to the directive, `pnpm verify:plan` names the strip and settings suites to rerun, and a `pnpm app:drive` shot of the flag's tooltip is the match-after-drawing evidence.
