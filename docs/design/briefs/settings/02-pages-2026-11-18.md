## The three structures

Each was drawn as a static HTML mock in the adopted tokens, with the product's own controls and values, a few pages each, at 1400×900.

| | Structure | A page is |
|---|---|---|
| A | navigation by when a setting bites in a run: before, while, after, this application | a ledger: label, value, where the value comes from, the explanation closed until asked for |
| B | Setup first (what blocks a run, what differs from defaults); nine entries | a list and a detail pane for anything there are several of; two columns of grouped rows for values |
| C | six long pages, a search field, one global workspace/global switch, an outline | label, control and explanation on one line |

The reviewer chose B with grafts from A and C, and said none was buildable as drawn: all three overflowed at the minimum window, and none drew the Extensions pages. Named against A: no place for Plugins, Tools or traceability; explanations behind a glyph with no accessible name; nothing for first use.

## The owner's choice

A, with B's Setup page; then less text, explanations and origins behind icons. Two revisions of the mock answered the reviewer's findings on A before the build: every page drawn and each of the thirteen categories given a place; the explanation control named, with a page-level control that opens all; Setup and attention marks for first use; rows that stack below 1180 px.

One thing the owner chose against the reviewer and against the design language's "state twice": a toggle carries no On/Off word and a default value no word at all. The toggle's position, its `aria-checked` and the glyphs' accessible names carry the state.

## What was built

The thirteen pages of `PD-CONFIG-012`, composed from the parts the categories already were, so every control, label, range, confirmation and command is the one it was. Measured in the built app on the scripted dialect, at 1400×900 and at 1000×680: `scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth` on the body of all thirteen pages at both sizes.

- No test and no proof was run on this machine; the proofs were repointed by reading ([the note](../../../defects/the-settings-proofs-were-repointed-to-the-pages-by-reading-and-not-run.md)).
