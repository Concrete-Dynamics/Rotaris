# `resilience-proof`

What this proof measures and re-verifies. The [proof registry](README.md) carries its area, prerequisites,
platforms, flags and neighbours; the script is [`apps/desktop/scripts/resilience-proof.mjs`](../../apps/desktop/scripts/resilience-proof.mjs).

The model ladder, the waits, the fallbacks, against scripted faults, and (`quota-wait`, OQ-173) that a provider-limit wait past the stall threshold is logged as a wait (at `INFO` since OQ-127's ruling, the scenario's session log kept at `INFO` to read it) and raises no `stall` issue, and (`override`, REQ-MONITOR-194/303, OQ-186) that the wait header's change-model chooser lists the pair the quota answer put out of quota with its reason and choosable, and (REQ-CHAT-006) that the composer's model chip then names the override in force, and (`switch-back`, OQ-177) that an entry switch back to the configured key after a same-tier move applies from the next iteration as its row says, and (`quota-retry`, REQ-MONITOR-194, since 2026-10-02) that the waiting pair chosen on the quota-wait surface clears its exclusion and is retried, re-recorded on a further quota response, and REQ-NOTIFY-060's authentication class (since 2026-10-02): (`fatal`) that a 401 with no usable fallback, after the automatic fallback found none, opens REQ-NOTIFY-072's 560 px recovery dialog in its own window, whose Cancel leaves the run failed and its notice standing and whose Retry restarts the run on the chosen model, a chosen model that fails too reopening it; (`reauth`) that its masked key field stores a new key — validated against a loopback catalog stand-in — and restarts the run on the same model with it; (`auth-fallback`) that a first 401 with `fallback_model` set pins it and restarts on it, no dialog opening — in plan mode too, the plan cycle restarted on the fallback — and opens the non-modal re-authentication prompt with the composer, the title bar's controls and the notice's buttons usable beside it and no control under it, measured at the opening size and at 1000x680 with an API-key provider's key field, and beside an expanded plan gate as its bar with the gate's three answers usable; (`auth-fallback-dialog`) that the fallback refused too opens the dialog naming it, closes the prompt and offers neither model that failed; (`auth-fallback-revise`) that a revise cycle refused on a first 401 is restarted as the same revise, its changes reaching the fallback and no prompt re-submitted; and (`generic`) that a failure of no earlier class raises REQ-NOTIFY-064's notice whose action returns to the workspace view; and (in `auth-fallback`, `auth-fallback-dialog` and `generic`, since finacc2's A1, REQ-NOTIFY-032/058) that main never reads the failure of the session the window started as another session's, and that no REQ-NOTIFY-032 warning stands beside the prompt after the fallback restart; and (`next-run-choice`, fin10) that the composer's next-run model chip clears the exclusion of the pair it chooses (REQ-MONITOR-194/146) — since fin11 the excluded key it already shows, chosen directly — and that the inspector's Model field names the key the root runs on (beta) during the held run (REQ-MONITOR-052/066); and (`child-timeout-inspector`, finacc F7, REQ-MONITOR-052/066/049) that a child retried on its role's fallback after REQ-ORCH-088's timeout is drawn in the inspector on beta, against beta's maximum input, while the retry runs, and on alpha, against alpha's, before; and (in `same-tier` and `two-waits`, PD-MONITOR-007, since 2026-10-06) that the helper calls — the intent classifier and the post-run analyst — follow the session's override and make no call on a pair held out of quota; and (`keep-waiting`, PD-MONITOR-004 revision 2, since 2026-10-06) that the wait header's Keep waiting in an out-of-quota wait retries the waiting pair at once, its exclusion cleared and no override persisted, and that a second press in the same frame retries nothing more and switches no model; and (`switch-over-override`, PD-ORCH-013 and PD-MONITOR-006, since 2026-10-06) that an entry switch from the run header onto a pair an earlier wait overrode takes the override back, the next iteration runs on the pair chosen, and the row says the switch holds for the rest of this run;
