### PD-MONITOR-008 — Workspace compact mode's 1180 px is the view's own width, not the viewport's

The width that REQ-MONITOR-051 compares against 1180 px MUST be the Workspace view's own width, as REQ-SHELL-059 measures it, and MUST NOT be the viewport's or the window's. Choosing an agent SHALL open the inspector as an overlay drawer exactly when the Workspace view is in compact mode under REQ-SHELL-059, and at no other width. Every other clause of REQ-MONITOR-051 stands as written: the inspector drawer and the agents/todos drawer remain mutually exclusive, and the workspace remains usable down to 1000×680.

**Why, and what it costs.** Two requirements named different measures for one threshold: REQ-SHELL-059 defines compact mode on the Workspace view's own width, evaluated on every resize and once after construction, while REQ-MONITOR-051 states one consequence of the mode against "a viewport width of 1180 px". The specification's own resolution (domain 12, row 13) makes the 1180 px compact threshold normative under REQ-SHELL-059 alone, and the view sits beside the navigation rail (68 px in the default theme, REQ-SHELL-129; wider under REQ-SHELL-029), so the two readings differ for every window between 1180 px and 1180 px plus the rail. Under the viewport reading the line the view docks at moves with the rail's width, docking a view as narrow as 1180 px minus the rail — about 1112 px in the default theme, less for the operator whose larger type scale widened the rail — which REQ-SHELL-059's own measure never admits; REQ-SHELL-061's 850 px and REQ-SHELL-065's 700 px are figures at their named sizes (OQ-525) and judge neither reading. The cost is that operators with a window in that band get overlay drawers where they had docked panes, the delegation tree one toggle away rather than beside the transcript (whether that is acceptable below the line is OQ-526's question); the gain is that the same space always gets the same arrangement, whatever the theme. The built `useNarrow` read the viewport from REQ-MONITOR-051's wording, a choice made in code, not in the record; it moves to the view's width under `debug-ui`, keeping the rule that the width is read when the observer attaches, never only on a change event (guide 14 §6 item 6). Ruled in [OQ-524](../../archive/architecture/open-questions/71-spikes-and-later-questions-38.md#oq-524).

Its case on REQ-MONITOR-051 is the width the threshold is measured on, and is disjoint from [`PD-SHELL-016`](pd-shell-016.md#pd-shell-016)'s (revision 2, 2026-11-14), which is where the agents and task-plan pane is drawn while the navigation sidebar is open.
