## The final step belt ends a working child as succeeded with no final response

**Outcome: repaired by [PD-ORCH-017](../../product/directives.md#pd-orch-017) (R-073, commit `3e131016`, 2026-10-16); confirmed on every child route and closed 2026-10-21 by `loc1-step-belt`.** The directive removed the belt for every delegated child, and the duress run above ran before it landed. Every child turn is built in one place, the `turn` closure of `IterationChildren.execute` in `packages/engine/src/children/orchestration.ts`, which passes `maxStepsPerTurn: null`; `runTurn` in `packages/engine/src/run/turn.ts` skips the belt for null, and the belt is the only source of a `partial` turn report the engine writes itself. No other construction of a child turn exists: the root's turn (`run.ts`) alone takes `RunConfig.maxStepsPerTurn` (100, ADR-0059 decision 8, unchanged), and the summariser, the intent classifier, the improvements analyst, compression and the run host's loop each make single backend calls with no step loop. So no route still settles a belt-ended child as `succeeded` with an empty `final_response`. Pinned in `packages/engine/src/children/children.test.ts`: the fresh child's case from `3e131016`, and the block "PD-ORCH-017 (REQ-ORCH-088, REQ-ORCH-112): no route into a child's turn carries a fixed model-step cap", six cases each giving one child turn more than 100 model steps: a follow-up by `session_id`, a nested child and its parent's turn resumed with its report, a child resumed from its record after a pause, a continuation of a child cut off while making progress, REQ-ORCH-095's re-prompt and REQ-NOTIFY-111's corrective turn. Each requires the child `succeeded` with its last answer as `final_response` and no belt warning. With `maxStepsPerTurn: 100` put back in the closure, all seven cases fail. Each turn stops at step 101, and on every one of the six routes the child settles as this note describes: state `succeeded`, report status `succeeded`, `final_response` empty. REQ-ORCH-088's fallback retry after the hard timeout runs through the same closure, and is pinned on its own in `packages/engine/src/run/ladder.test.ts` ("PD-ORCH-017 (REQ-ORCH-088, REQ-ORCH-112): a child retried on the role's fallback after its hard timeout works past 100 model steps in the retry", added by the review), which fails the same way with the cap put back. The root keeps its belt, and what the person is told when it fires is not changed here: no requirement names the belt (the Specs section below), so nothing more is owed or recorded.

A turn that exceeds 100 model steps is ended as `partial` (the final step belt, ADR-0059 decision 8). When that turn is a child's, the child's settlement maps every turn that did not fail to `succeeded`, so a child cut mid-work is reported succeeded, with an empty `final_response`, and the coordinator receives a success with nothing in it.

Seen for the first time live in the duress run at `cb6d1d5` (2026-10-15), session `1e7349db`. The child `wave2-reconcile-docs` had worked 28.5 minutes and 127 tool calls; its last calls were a `haet_edit` and a `read_file`, and its last text announced a check it was about to run. At 13:54:59.958 the belt ended its turn. The child row reads state `succeeded`, report status `succeeded`, `final_response` empty, and its brief's deliverable ("a list of every disagreement") was never produced. The only trace is one session-log warning that names no agent ("the turn exceeded 100 model steps and was ended as partial (the final step belt fired)"); no issue and no timeline entry record it.

The pack lives in an ephemeral cloud container (`Rotaris-crw-wt-live2/.live-run/runs/2026-10-15T13-20-16-026Z`) and may be gone; the figures above are the evidence.

## Specs

`REQ-ORCH-112` provides the `partial` report status for a child that stops before its brief is done, which this report contradicts. `REQ-ORCH-100` makes the raw-count thresholds the run's backstop; the belt has no requirement or configuration key of its own, so whether a working child may be cut at a fixed step count, and what the person is told, may need an open question before code. `REQ-ORCH-088` for the child's settlement.
