## 3. Content model and hierarchy

Two regions, one object: the agents of the focused session. **The graph card** ranks hierarchy and control: the run, then what it delegated, then what can be done to the selection. Its one primary is the run row; its one destructive control is "Cancel selection". **The table card** ranks the per-agent figures; its one primary per row is the agent's name, and its two controls are the row's last cell. The selection relates them: one selection, marked in both (REQ-MONITOR-042). Nothing is disclosed on demand. Difficult data: twenty agents (REQ-MONITOR-285), a persona of 24 characters and more (cut with an ellipsis, its title whole), a tree three levels deep, a stalled and a timed-out agent (their tags stand in the state cell).

## 5. Interaction

Selection is a click on a tree row, the run row or a table row; Enter and Space on the run row. With `interface/agentPopout` on, a click on a tree row also opens the agent's persona window, as it did on Mission (priced in the directive). Tab order: the view's header, the metric cards (none focusable), the table's rows' controls, the sessions card, the divider, then the graph card: run row, tree rows, Pause, Cancel selection, Open, "Open Runtime settings". Cancel is confirmed in a `ConfirmDialog` that names the agent and what cascades; nothing else is irreversible. The palette reaches the view by "Open Overview" and by the words that found Mission.

## 7. Accessibility obligations

The two cards are regions named "Delegation graph" and "Agent activity". The table keeps its table semantics in both forms because every row, cell and header carries its role in the markup; in the narrow form the header row is out of sight and still in the accessibility tree, and the three figures carry their column's words as visible text. State is a dot and a word (REQ-SHELL-149). The pause explanation is the button's accessible description. **Not verified:** no screen reader was run over either form.
