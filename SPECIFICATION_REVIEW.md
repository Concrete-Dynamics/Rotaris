# Specification review — proposed decisions

Reviewed 2026-09-06 against commit `4ee75c62dc54927965f44a1807f47030e51450cb`.

Status: **accepted 2026-09-06; implementation in progress**. The user accepted all 48 recommendations, permitting evidence-led adjustments. Changes remain uncommitted; commits and pushes are deferred until the goal is finished. Execution evidence and remaining work are tracked in [SPECIFICATION_RESOLUTION_PROGRESS.md](SPECIFICATION_RESOLUTION_PROGRESS.md).

The review contains **48 items**: nine additional findings, followed by a disposition for each of the **39 questions still carried open** in the architecture register. Some dispositions concern architecture ownership, measurement, or a stale premise; their kind is stated explicitly. Each row proposes a concrete resolution.

Coverage: a structural scan of all 21 specification domains; semantic review of the complete specification-question register, its relevant owning requirements, resolution records, approval gates, and targeted cross-domain contracts. The findings below are established within that scope. Further sentence-level review of the corpus may identify additional issues.

Structural result: **4,398 unique requirement definitions**, including **444 identifiers in the two retired domains**, hence **3,954 in shipping domains**. Every `REQ-*` reference found in the domain documents resolves to a definition. These counts include withdrawn identifiers. Runtime tests were outside this review.
