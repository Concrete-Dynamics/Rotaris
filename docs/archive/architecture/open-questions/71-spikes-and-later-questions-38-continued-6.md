**OQ-552 — How does the download notice leave the banner when nothing replaces it — the user's dismissal of UD-3, or a background transport failure UD-4 keeps silent?** *(PD-STARTUP-008 revision 2 UD-3 and UD-4, PD-SHELL-005 revision 2, REQ-STARTUP-118, REQ-STARTUP-179, REQ-NOTIFY-040, REQ-SHELL-150. Raised 2026-11-21 by the `design-ui` brief of the update's surfaces ([`title-bar-and-rail.md`](../../../design/briefs/title-bar-and-rail.md) §10 item 2), for [R-117](../../../product/requests.md#r-117).)*

**What is open.** UD-3 says "dismissing the download notice … only hides it", which presumes a dismiss control. PD-SHELL-005 revision 2 and REQ-STARTUP-118 keep the download notice **not persistent**, and REQ-NOTIFY-040 gives a non-persistent notice no dismiss control and only two exits: replacement and a run-start clear. UD-4 hides a transport failure of a download the user did not start, so the notice that showed its progress has no outcome face to become, and no exit: it would keep a stale figure (REQ-SHELL-150).

**What the build proceeds under.** The download notice stays non-persistent, with no Dismiss (PD-SHELL-005); a silent transport failure replaces it in place with REQ-STARTUP-116's offer and `Update now`, as a cancel does (UD-2), and the next launch downloads again (UD-4).

**What decides it.** Either the download notice becomes persistent while it runs, so UD-3's dismissal has a control and REQ-STARTUP-118's "non-persistent" is narrowed; or UD-3's "download notice" is read as the ready notice alone. And, separately, the face a silent transport failure leaves: the offer (the reading built), or a clear that REQ-NOTIFY-040 would have to admit. Not ruled here: the writer is not the orchestrator.

**Ruled 2026-11-21 under the owner's delegation ([R-072](../../../product/requests.md#r-072)), for [R-117](../../../product/requests.md#r-117).** Advocate A and Advocate B each recommended that the download notice be persistent while it runs, its Dismiss only hiding it (UD-3) with no version recorded and the title-bar indicator carrying the state; and that a transport failure UD-4 keeps silent turn the notice, while it is still the current one, into REQ-STARTUP-116's offer with `Update now`, and publish nothing where it is not. Advocate B added a test pinning that the dismissal never reaches REQ-NOTIFY-217's recording path. Ruled as both recommend, with B's test. UD-3 is the owner's own ruling of 2026-11-21 and names a dismissal of the download notice and, beside it, of the ready notice, so "download notice" is not the ready notice alone; REQ-NOTIFY-040 gives a dismiss control exactly to a notice whose `persistent` flag is set, so the owner's later clause can be met only by setting it. PD-SHELL-005's "SHALL NOT be persistent while the download runs" (OQ-85, confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-06) gave as its reason "so no dismissal can wave off a version whose install is in flight", and UD-3 removes that hazard itself by making the dismissal record nothing. The consortium therefore carries the owner's later clause into the earlier directive and rules nothing against either: [`PD-SHELL-005`](../../../product/directives/pd-shell-005.md#pd-shell-005) revision 3, which REQ-STARTUP-118 joins for its "non-persistent", the falsified reason kept beside the new one. From now the build proceeds under this: (1) the download notice is persistent for the whole of the transfer and the verification; `Cancel download` stays its one action (PD-SHELL-005 revision 2), and its Dismiss hides it — the download runs on, `updates/dismissedVersion` is untouched, and the indicator stays (PD-SHELL-018); a test pins that dismissing the update notice in its downloading or ready face never reaches the waved-off recording of REQ-STARTUP-117 and REQ-NOTIFY-217. (2) A hidden or replaced download notice is not published again by a later repaint or phase ([OQ-554](#oq-554)). (3) A transport failure of a download the user did not start: while the update notice is current it becomes, in place, REQ-STARTUP-116's offer — its title, the summary or the fallback, `Update now`, persistent, its Dismiss waving the version off (REQ-STARTUP-117 as UD-3 leaves it) — and no download of that version starts on its own again in that process; where the notice is not current nothing is published; either way the indicator leaves, the failure is logged, and the next launch downloads again (UD-4). The offer states no failure, so UD-4's "is not shown" holds; the download face left standing would state progress that is no longer true (REQ-SHELL-150), and a clear is not one of REQ-NOTIFY-040's exits. What it costs: a Dismiss stands beside `Cancel download` on one notice and the two mean different things, hiding and stopping, which REQ-STARTUP-118's fixed message cannot say; the indicator staying after the Dismiss is the evidence, and the brief records it for the audit ([`notice-banner.md`](../../../design/briefs/notice-banner.md) §10). It reopens on a measured user who dismissed meaning to cancel, or on the owner's reading of UD-3 against PD-SHELL-005 differing from this one.

**OQ-553 — Once the portable or the AppImage has been replaced in place on verification (UD-8), is its ready notice REQ-STARTUP-119's `Update installed` or REQ-STARTUP-174's ready notice?** *(REQ-STARTUP-119 as PD-STARTUP-008 narrows it, REQ-STARTUP-173, REQ-STARTUP-174, UD-8. Raised 2026-11-21 by the same brief, §10 item 3.)*

**What is open.** REQ-STARTUP-174 applies "once a pending update has been verified — or, where REQ-STARTUP-173 permits, already applied in place" and words the notice as a version "that it will be installed the next time Rotaris starts". UD-8 now swaps the portable's and the AppImage's target as soon as it is verified, so that sentence is false there. REQ-STARTUP-119's narrowing keeps "the in-place replacement's `Update installed` with `Restart now`".

**What the build proceeds under.** An in-place flavour shows `Update installed` with `Restart now` and a message that the version runs at the next start (the brief's §6 row 3′); the installed flavour shows REQ-STARTUP-174's ready notice.

**What decides it.** One reading of REQ-STARTUP-174's "or … already applied in place": the ready notice with words that fit an applied update, or REQ-STARTUP-119's title for that case. Not ruled here.

**Ruled 2026-11-21 under the owner's delegation ([R-072](../../../product/requests.md#r-072)), for [R-117](../../../product/requests.md#r-117).** Advocate A and Advocate B each recommended the title `Update installed`, the action `Restart now` and the message "Rotaris {version} has been downloaded, verified and installed. It runs the next time Rotaris starts, or now with Restart now."; A read this as no departure, "will be installed" meaning "takes effect" in the in-place case REQ-STARTUP-174 names, and B as a departure from REQ-STARTUP-174's phrase, to be named. Ruled as both on the face and as B on the record. REQ-STARTUP-174 reaches the in-place case by its own words ("or … already applied in place") and asks its notice to convey that the version "will be installed the next time Rotaris starts", which UD-8, the owner's, makes false there; reading a conveyed fact as another fact is a departure written as a reading, so [`PD-STARTUP-008`](../../../product/directives/pd-startup-008.md#pd-startup-008) revision 3 narrows REQ-STARTUP-174 for the in-place case. The title is REQ-STARTUP-119's clause that PD-STARTUP-008 kept ("The in-place replacement's `Update installed` with `Restart now` still governs"), and it is the only title that keeps REQ-STARTUP-121 met: `Restart now` is offered "on the `Update installed` notice and on no other", PD-STARTUP-008 item 4 adding only the installed flavour's ready notice. From now the build proceeds under this: once the portable or the AppImage has been replaced in place (UD-8), the update notice becomes a persistent success notice titled `Update installed`, message `Rotaris {version} has been downloaded, verified and installed. It runs the next time Rotaris starts, or now with Restart now.`, action `Restart now` with REQ-STARTUP-174's guards; it is REQ-STARTUP-174's ready notice, so its Dismiss only hides it (UD-3). The installed flavour keeps REQ-STARTUP-174's ready notice as the brief words it. The indicator's ready description says the same. When the notice is published at all is [OQ-554](#oq-554)'s. What it costs: nothing in code beyond one face; the two ready notices differ in title, which is the specification's own split.

**OQ-554 — Which update transitions take the banner back from another notice: the ready state, the offer a cancel from the title-bar indicator leaves, a failure?** *(PD-SHELL-018, PD-STARTUP-008 UD-2, UD-4 and UD-10, REQ-NOTIFY-024, REQ-STARTUP-174, REQ-STARTUP-179, REQ-STARTUP-150. Raised 2026-11-21 by the same brief, §10 item 5.)*

**What is open.** The banner holds one notice and the newest replaces it (REQ-NOTIFY-024). PD-SHELL-018 adds the indicator so the progress, the cancel and the restart stay reachable "while another notice holds" the slot, which reads as the update not taking it back. REQ-STARTUP-174 says the update notice "SHALL become" the ready notice, REQ-STARTUP-179 that a failed apply is "always visible", and UD-2 that a cancel's notice "becomes" the offer. The updater as built publishes a fresh notice when its own was replaced (`show` in `apps/desktop/src/main/updater/updater.ts`), so each transition would push out whatever the user was reading.

**What the build proceeds under.** Progress never re-takes the banner (it is the indicator's). A failure the requirements make visible publishes, replacing what stands. The ready state and the offer after a cancel from the indicator do not publish while another notice holds the slot: the indicator shows ready, and the cancel's own press is the user's acknowledgement.

**What decides it.** Per transition: publish and replace, or leave the slot and let the indicator carry it. Not ruled here.

**Ruled 2026-11-21 under the owner's delegation ([R-072](../../../product/requests.md#r-072)), for [R-117](../../../product/requests.md#r-117).** Advocate A and Advocate B each recommended that progress never publish, that the ready state never publish (changing the notice in place only while it is current), that the offer a cancel from the indicator leaves never publish, and that every failure the record makes visible — an integrity refusal under UD-4, a failed download the user started, a failed `Restart now` or next-start apply — publish and replace under the retained update identifier, `show` in `updater.ts` no longer publishing afresh otherwise; on REQ-NOTIFY-024, seams §6, PD-SHELL-018, REQ-NOTIFY-003 and REQ-STARTUP-179. A found no departure; B found REQ-NOTIFY-222's "publishes" borderline and, for the cancel, that the indicator's description must then say the version downloads again at the next start. Ruled as both, with B on REQ-NOTIFY-222 and on the description, and with one case added for the offer. **The rule:** (a) while the update notice — its identifier retained under REQ-NOTIFY-217 — is the current notice, every transition changes it in place; where it is not, a transition publishes only (b) a failure, replacing whatever holds the slot (REQ-NOTIFY-024, REQ-NOTIFY-003), for every failure the record makes visible: a refusal on authenticity or integrity and a discard UD-4 shows, a transport failure of a download the user started, and a failed apply by `Restart now` or at a next start (REQ-STARTUP-179, REQ-STARTUP-150); or (c) the offer a cancel leaves, only where no notice holds the slot. Otherwise nothing is published and the indicator alone carries the state (PD-SHELL-018). **Per transition:** progress repaints in place only; ready becomes the ready notice in place only, else the indicator shows ready with `Restart now`; the offer after a cancel from the indicator is in place where the notice is current, published where the slot is empty, and otherwise not shown, the version held for the process and downloaded again at the next launch (UD-2), which the indicator's cancel description says; the offer after a silent transport failure is [OQ-552](#oq-552)'s, in place or nothing; a shown failure publishes. **Why (c) departs from both advocates:** they withheld the offer so that the update never takes a slot another notice holds; where the slot is empty — the download notice having been hidden (UD-3) — publishing displaces nothing and keeps UD-2's `Update now` reachable in that process, so their reason does not reach that case. **UD-2 is the owner's,** and is not rewritten: it fixes what the cancel leaves, the offer, held for the process, nothing recorded; once another notice has replaced the update notice, REQ-NOTIFY-024 says the replaced notice "does not return unprompted", and UD-10 and PD-SHELL-018, the owner's of the same day, put the cancel in the title bar for exactly that case. Read together the update's own transitions do not take back a slot the person is reading; UD-2 says nothing of that case, and (c) fills it. **The departures:** REQ-STARTUP-174's "the update notice SHALL become", REQ-NOTIFY-222's "publishes" and REQ-STARTUP-116's "a … notice appears", each where the update notice is not current, are narrowed by [`PD-STARTUP-008`](../../../product/directives/pd-startup-008.md#pd-startup-008) revision 3 (REQ-NOTIFY-222 and REQ-STARTUP-116 already in its Narrows list, REQ-STARTUP-174 joining it). From now the build proceeds under this: `show` publishes afresh only in (b) and (c); an in-place change whose notice is no longer current publishes nothing; the indicator's state is emitted before the notice for one transition (the channel, [`title-bar-and-rail.md`](../../../design/briefs/title-bar-and-rail.md) §3.1); the transitions are tabled in [`notice-banner.md`](../../../design/briefs/notice-banner.md) §4.1. What it costs: a person who replaced the download notice and never looks at the title bar learns of the ready state only at the next start, where it is applied anyway (REQ-STARTUP-173); a person who cancels from the indicator while another notice stands has no `Update now` in that process. It reopens on a measured case of a ready update missed for a day, or on a request that the ready state interrupt.

**OQ-555 — Does REQ-STARTUP-120's `Try again` stand on an integrity refusal that REQ-STARTUP-179 says is not retried for that release by the same process?** *(REQ-STARTUP-120, REQ-STARTUP-123, REQ-STARTUP-179, UD-4. Raised 2026-11-21 by the same brief, §10 item 7.)*

**What is open.** UD-4 shows every refusal on authenticity or integrity as REQ-STARTUP-120's notice, whose action is `Try again` and whose message says "You can try again". REQ-STARTUP-179 says such a refusal "SHALL NOT be retried for that release by the same process". Whether a press of `Try again` is the retry 179 forbids, or 179 forbids only an automatic one, is not said.

**What the build proceeds under.** `Try again` is offered on a transport failure the user sees and on a failed `Restart now` or next-start apply; on an integrity refusal the notice keeps REQ-STARTUP-120's title and message and carries no action.

**What decides it.** Either 179 binds the product's own retries only, and `Try again` stands everywhere; or it binds the user's too, and REQ-STARTUP-120's message and action are narrowed for an integrity refusal. Not ruled here.

**Ruled 2026-11-21 under the owner's delegation ([R-072](../../../product/requests.md#r-072)), for [R-117](../../../product/requests.md#r-117).** Advocate A and Advocate B each recommended that REQ-STARTUP-179 bind the user's retry too: an integrity or authenticity refusal shows `Update not installed` with no action, departing from REQ-STARTUP-120 and REQ-NOTIFY-221 for that case under a revision of PD-STARTUP-008, `Try again` staying on a shown transport failure and a failed apply. A's message was "Rotaris is unchanged. You can download the release manually."; B's was "Rotaris is unchanged. The download could not be verified, so nothing was installed.", with no release-page action, a manual download from a feed that failed verification skipping the verification. Both cautioned that UD-4's "shown as REQ-STARTUP-120's notice" is the owner's, and both read it as fixing the surface. **UD-4 judged first:** it answers the question REQ-STARTUP-179 reserved to the owner — "whether … each of those refusals, is shown, and on which surface" — and 179 itself, in the same breath as "SHALL NOT be retried for that release by the same process", already named "REQ-STARTUP-120's notice with the technical sentence as its detail"; a phrase that sits beside the prohibition in one requirement names the notice (its title, severity, persistence and details), not the `Try again` that requirement forbids. So UD-4 fixes the surface, the wording is the consortium's, and no escalation is raised. **Ruled:** REQ-STARTUP-179's "SHALL NOT be retried for that release by the same process" names no initiator and sits beside "SHALL offer no override" (REQ-STARTUP-123); a press of `Try again` after a digest mismatch would fetch the same release from the same feed in the same process, which is the retry it forbids. The message is A's, the narrowest departure: REQ-STARTUP-120's message with "try again or" removed, and every other fact REQ-NOTIFY-221 asks for kept. B's case against a manual download was weighed and not followed: REQ-STARTUP-120 names "the checksum-mismatch sentence" as its example detail beside "download the release manually", so the source requirement offers a manual download after exactly this refusal, and the release page is not offered as an action, that member being PD-SHELL-018's for a link-only build. From now the build proceeds under this: a refusal on authenticity or integrity, and every discard UD-4 shows, publishes a persistent error notice titled `Update not installed`, message `Rotaris is unchanged. You can download the release manually.`, the technical sentence as copyable details, and no action; `Try again` stays on every other failure REQ-STARTUP-120 governs, re-running what failed as that requirement states. [`PD-STARTUP-008`](../../../product/directives/pd-startup-008.md#pd-startup-008) revision 3 narrows REQ-STARTUP-120 and REQ-NOTIFY-221 for that case. What it costs: a refusal caused by a corrupted transfer rather than a bad release can be retried only by the next launch. It reopens if the owner reads UD-4 as fixing REQ-STARTUP-120's words as well, which would make the message an escalation.

**OQ-556 — Is "open the page of an available release" a member of REQ-NOTIFY-044's action vocabulary, which PD-SHELL-018 does not name?** *(PD-SHELL-018, PD-STARTUP-014, REQ-SHELL-115, REQ-NOTIFY-044 as PD-STARTUP-008 narrows it. Raised 2026-11-21 by the same brief, §10 item 8.)*

**What is open.** PD-SHELL-018 adds the action to REQ-SHELL-115's closed list. REQ-NOTIFY-044 states the same vocabulary from the notice side, and PD-STARTUP-008 narrowed it with one member and "No other action is added, and the closed property … still govern[s]". `NoticeActionSchema` (`packages/contracts/src/notices.ts`) implements both lists as one union, so the member cannot be added to one and not the other.

**What the build proceeds under.** The member is added once, parameterless, with main holding and validating the page (PD-STARTUP-014), and REQ-NOTIFY-044 is read as narrowed by the same case.

**What decides it.** A back-pointer on REQ-NOTIFY-044 naming PD-SHELL-018 (a revision of the directive's Narrows list), or a ruling that the two lists differ. Not ruled here.

**Ruled 2026-11-21 under the owner's delegation ([R-072](../../../product/requests.md#r-072)), for [R-117](../../../product/requests.md#r-117).** Advocate A and Advocate B each recommended adding one parameterless `open-release-page` member to REQ-NOTIFY-044 as well, `NoticeActionSchema` being one union and REQ-SHELL-115 one identifier per result; main holding the page and validating it (an `https` address on the feed's host, the feed repository's releases path, no user information, no port but the default) and opening it with `shell.openExternal`; a press with no page held logging one line and doing nothing; no address from a renderer; and a revision of PD-SHELL-018 naming REQ-NOTIFY-044, disjoint from PD-STARTUP-008's cancel and naming it (OQ-237). Against PD-STARTUP-008's "exactly one member", both answered that PD-SHELL-018 is the owner's later decision of the same day naming the second. Ruled as both recommend. The two lists are one vocabulary seen from the banner and from the notice, and the owner's decision "REQ-SHELL-115's closed list gains … open the page of an available release, and no other" decides the member for both; PD-STARTUP-008 item 10's "exactly one member" states its own case, the cancel, which a directive narrowing by a disjoint case leaves whole. No owner clause is reworded: [`PD-SHELL-018`](../../../product/directives/pd-shell-018.md#pd-shell-018) revision 2 adds REQ-NOTIFY-044 to its Narrows list with its back-pointer, and PD-STARTUP-008's note on REQ-NOTIFY-044 gains a dated line. From now the build proceeds under this: one member `open-release-page`, no parameter, valid in the banner only on the link-only offer of PD-STARTUP-014; main keeps the page beside the offer and checks it as above at the press, opens it in the default browser, and logs and ignores a press for an offer it no longer holds. What it costs: nothing beyond what PD-SHELL-018 already priced.

**OQ-557 — Do the notice banner and the acknowledgement stack take a row in `docs/design/README.md` §7, so a brief of their states has a slug?** *(REQ-SHELL-112, REQ-SHELL-114, G1 of §4.2, `design-ui` step 1, OQ-545's precedent. Raised 2026-11-21 by the same brief, §10 item 9.)*

The update's surfaces are carried in the title bar's brief (`title-bar-and-rail`), where only the indicator belongs.

**What the build proceeds under.** The brief stands at `docs/design/briefs/title-bar-and-rail.md` and covers the update's faces of the notice and the acknowledgement beside the indicator.

**What decides it.** A §7 row "Notice banner, acknowledgements" (mockup section `toast` for the second, none for the first), whose brief the update's faces would move to; or the title bar's row extended to name both. Not ruled here.

**Ruled 2026-11-21 under the owner's delegation ([R-072](../../../product/requests.md#r-072)), for [R-117](../../../product/requests.md#r-117).** Advocate A and Advocate B each recommended a §7 row "Notice banner, acknowledgements", mockup section `toast` for the stack and none for the banner, nearest code `Notice.tsx` and `Acknowledgements.tsx`, the update's faces of the notice (R1) and the acknowledgement (R4) moving to its brief while the title bar's brief keeps the indicator and its action (R2, R3); A proposed the slug `notices`, B `notice-banner`. Ruled as both, with B's slug: a §7 slug names its row's first surface (`title-bar-and-rail`, `agent-list`, `composer`), and `notices` would read as every notice's copy rather than the banner's drawing. Unlike OQ-545, the surface exists and is drawn: REQ-SHELL-114 and REQ-SHELL-112 fix it, `Notice.tsx` and `Acknowledgements.tsx` draw it, and the row changes no structure the specification fixes, so no directive is needed. Done in this ruling's commit: the row in [`docs/design/README.md` §7](../../../design/README/07-7-fidelity-consult-before-maintain-after.md), the slug in `design-ui`'s table, R1 and R4 moved to [`docs/design/briefs/notice-banner.md`](../../../design/briefs/notice-banner.md), and [`title-bar-and-rail.md`](../../../design/briefs/title-bar-and-rail.md) keeping R2 and R3.
