# REQ-102 - Filter the list and count what is left

The list is shown whole, or narrowed to what is still to do, or to what is done, and the count of what is left
is always on screen.

## Acceptance criteria

- The three filters are `all`, `active` and `done`.
- The count names the todos that are not done, whichever filter is showing.
