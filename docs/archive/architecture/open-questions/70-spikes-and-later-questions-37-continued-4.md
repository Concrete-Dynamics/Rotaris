**OQ-522 — Should the composer's primary button become stop while a run is live, where REQ-CHAT-005 makes it `Queue`?** *(REQ-CHAT-005, REQ-CHAT-029, PD-ORCH-005. Raised 2026-11-03 by [R-093](../../../product/requests.md#r-093), guide 06 §8 item 8 ([the guide](../../../research/design/06-ux-patterns-for-agent-products.md)) of the design research corpus and backlog row 31 ([the backlog](../../../research/design/backlog.md)); numbered from OQ-493: OQ-462 to OQ-492 are the hardening orchestration's.)*

**What is open.** REQ-CHAT-005: the button reads “`Queue` while a local run is active”. Stopping is already reachable from the composer as `/stop` (REQ-CHAT-029, narrowed by PD-ORCH-005 clause 15, “the stop control's gesture”) and from the stop control. Guide 06 §8 D06-8 cites the convention that the control that starts work becomes stop in the same place.

**What the build proceeds under.** REQ-CHAT-005's `Queue`, with stop through `/stop` and the stop control.

**What decides it.** One ruling: REQ-CHAT-005 stands, or a directive over it puts stop in the button and moves queuing elsewhere.

**Ruled 2026-11-03 under the owner's delegation (R-072).** Advocate A (the user's lens) recommended that REQ-CHAT-005 stand as PD-CHAT-001 narrows it, with stop left in REQ-SESSION-186's control beside the pill and no directive; Advocate B (the system's lens) recommended the same, as a confirmation of what is built. Ruled: REQ-CHAT-005 stands and the question is read as already met. The send pill reads `Queue` while a local run is active, and stopping is reached from the composer by the stop control and by `/stop` (REQ-CHAT-029 as PD-ORCH-005 clause 15 narrows it). Three grounds decide it. First, the pill's label is the owner's text: PD-CHAT-001, raised by the owner (R-010) and revised by the owner three times (R-040, R-041, R-064, the last the owner's own ruling on OQ-98), says "The send pill SHALL carry the mode's short label visible — `Run`, `Continue`, `Queue`"; a directive putting stop in the pill would displace that sentence, not sit beside it as a disjoint case, so it is not the consortium's to mint. Second, the entry's premise that stopping is not reached where the run started is withdrawn by the same directive's strip paragraph: "Steer, pause, stop … are the same states the same controls already drew; only their shells move into the strip", and as built the stop control is a danger button in the strip immediately before the `Queue` pill whenever a run is active, reading `Stop run` and then `Force stop` while a stop is pending, as REQ-SESSION-186 ("the control MUST say which of the two the next activation will do") requires. One strip, one contour, one band: the guide's "in the same place" is met at the band, and only the exact slot differs. Third, a stop in the pill would harm the journey the record protects: REQ-CHAT-006 keeps the composer editable while a run is active, REQ-CHAT-001 submits on Enter, and the pill's `Queue` is how that text reaches the next iteration; a primary that stopped instead would turn Enter on a drafted follow-up into a graceful stop and a second press into REQ-SESSION-092's forced cancel, and §1.7 ("The primary button is the only filled surface in the system") would then carry the destructive meaning §1.3 reserves for `--rt-danger-500`. No requirement is departed from, so no directive is minted and §1 is not amended; the build proceeds under REQ-CHAT-005 as narrowed, and nothing executes. One fact is recorded for the reopening condition rather than ruled on: PD-CHAT-001's "what never shortens" names steer, pause and the send pill and not the stop control, which the build keeps labelled at every density but which the `tight` and `narrow` strips were never measured with stop in mind. What reopens the question: a `review-ui` critique of `pnpm app:drive` shots of the running strip at 1000×680 and below the 900 px and 700 px thresholds finding the stop control shortened, wrapped off the band or scrolled out of view, which is a `debug-ui` defect against PD-CHAT-001 and not a reason to move stop into the pill; a `run-autopsy` finding of a stop delayed by searching for the control, which reopens placement within the strip; or an owner's request to revise PD-CHAT-001's pill labels, which must also say where queuing goes and is logged in `docs/product/requests.md`.
