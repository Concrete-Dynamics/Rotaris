**OQ-523 — Is a `--rt-viz-2` persona label one stop from `--rt-run` a “state colour” under PD-CHAT-005?** *(PD-CHAT-005, §1.3. Raised 2026-11-03 by [R-093](../../../product/requests.md#r-093), guide 06 §8 item 15 ([the guide](../../../research/design/06-ux-patterns-for-agent-products.md)) of the design research corpus and backlog row 15 ([the backlog](../../../research/design/backlog.md)); numbered from OQ-493: OQ-462 to OQ-492 are the hardening orchestration's.)*

**What is open.** PD-CHAT-005, the owner's, takes persona colours “from the categorical set the adopted design language already declares … in that set's own order”. **What the build proceeds under.** PD-CHAT-005 and the persona ramp as adopted.

The harm the directive names, two facts "indistinguishable on a row that carries both", is bounded by §1.3's "Colour never carries a fact on its own" (`REQ-SHELL-149`, `REQ-TRACE-261`, `REQ-NOTIFY-258`): the persona label is the name in REQ-CHAT-057's role column, and the state beside it is a dot-plus-word or glyph-plus-word in another place, so neither depends on telling teal at 72 % from teal at 65 %. The reading cuts both ways: the two sets are disjoint by token, and a surface that drew a state in a `--rt-viz-*` token, or in the `-400` stop one of them aliases, would be a defect against the directive, not a reason to recolour the ramp. Guide 06 D06-15 reports another product's palette and is weight, not a ground. Nothing is displaced; no directive is minted, §1.3 is not amended (the entry asked for no note there, and the ramp stands), and nothing is built. The question asks whether the label is told from the dot without the word, which is a measurement this round cannot take: it reopens on a `review-ui` pass over a transcript row and a delegation-tree row carrying both a persona label and a running dot that finds the two read as one fact, or finds a state mark drawn with no word or glyph; on a §1.3 ramp amendment that moves `--rt-viz-1` to `--rt-viz-3` off the axis hues; or on the owner revising `PD-CHAT-005`. The remedy then is the owner's directive or a §1.3 amendment, never a quiet token swap in `Row.tsx`. Found beside the question and not ruled here: the tool row's pulsing `◉ running…` is drawn in `--rt-accent-300`, a stop of the done hue, which belongs in a defect note under `docs/defects/`.
