## 8. The 2026-11-14 revision

**Status:** decided 2026-11-14 and built on `feat/r-101-experience-redesign`; a branch and a pull request, not landed.
**How this part was made:** proposed and decided by the model (`claude-fable-5-2`) under [R-101](../../product/requests.md#r-101), in which the owner authorised a redesign of the visual language and of the information architecture, and the standing delegation [R-098](../../product/requests.md#r-098). The owner worded none of it. Where this part and [§1](01-1-the-design-language.md) disagree, this part governs: it is the later decision and it names what it replaces.

The structure it stands in is [the information-architecture map](../information-architecture.md), round 3. Its method was the Impeccable design skill's: the surface's mode decides how far ambition reaches, and this product's surfaces are all **Operate** — a person completing a task for hours — so the ambition is precision and restraint, and a pattern the operator already knows earns its place.

### 8.2 The decisions

**R1 — Planes: the content is lit and the chrome recedes.** The chrome — the title bar, the sidebar, the status strip, and the Workspace's side panes and dock — is the deepest plane; the view's ground stands above it; a surface above the ground means one of two things, *this floats* (a dialog, a menu, a drawer) or *this takes input* (the composer, a field). `--rt-chrome` moves from 23 % to 18 % in `rotaris-dim` and from 16 % to 11 % in `nocturne`, and from 10 % to 5 % in `high-contrast`. *Replaces* §1.1's "chrome a half-step above the ground".

**R2 — Graphite: the neutrals carry a trace of the accent's side of the wheel.** Every neutral of `rotaris-dim` and `nocturne` takes hue 275 at a chroma under 0.01. No lightness of a text or boundary token moves, and lightness is what the floors read: `static-contrast.ts` holds every pair on the new planes. `high-contrast` stays achromatic. *Replaces* §1.1's "true neutral gray".

**R3 — A region is a ruled section, not a card.** `.card` on a view's ground draws a hairline above the region and nothing else: no fill, no border around it, no radius. A card inside a floating surface keeps the surface. The Overview's four figures are one band ([`PD-MONITOR-010`](../../product/directives/pd-monitor-010.md#pd-monitor-010)). A section's heading, its table's column heads and its cells stand on one left edge. *Replaces* §1.7's card as the default container.

**R4 — A heading is a heading.** A section's heading is set in sentence case at `--rt-text-sm`, medium, in the primary ink. The upper-case tracked label stays where a line labels and does not head: a table's column heads, the sidebar's and the Settings list's groups, a plan's phases. *Replaces* §1.4's kicker as the card heading.

**R5 — One family in the interface.** A view's title is set in the body face at `--rt-h4`, medium (`--rt-h5` in the first build, which the first critique found barely above its subtitle). The display face is the wordmark's alone. *Replaces* §1.4's display face for view titles.

**R6 — An empty state is a statement and one next act.** No frame, no dot grid; the title in the primary ink, the hint at `--rt-text-sm` in the secondary. The motif (§1.10) is no longer drawn behind text. *Replaces* §1.10's use of the dot grid in an empty state.

**R7 — One page frame.** Every primary view but the Workspace is inset once, by the shell: `--rt-space-5` above, `--rt-space-6` at the sides and below. A view adds no inset of its own. The Workspace fills its region, as before.

**R8 — The browser's own surfaces are themed, as far as was safe.** The scrollbar thumb's hover takes a token where it took a literal. *Tried and withdrawn:* the caret and the native accent taking the accent through a rule on `:root`. With that one rule a proof's launch stopped presenting frames after a navigation and every step that waits on two frames ran seconds late; the cause inside the browser was not established, and the rule is recorded as [a defect note](../../defects/a-root-rule-setting-caret-color-and-accent-color-stops-a-proof-launch-presenting-frames.md) and not shipped.

### 8.3 What was weighed and not taken

| Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|
| Replace the violet accent with a neutral ink accent, so that colour means state alone | the accent is the Z axis of the product's own three-axis mark and doubles as the *done* state by design; splitting them means a second hue beside violet or a selection drawn in brightness alone, and the baseline showed no reading error that would pay for either |
| A light theme as the default | the use scene is long sessions beside a terminal and an editor, both dark for most of this audience; a light theme is a fourth theme to add, not a default to change |
| Replace the faces | the three faces are self-hosted and licensed, and the body face carries the interface well; what was wrong was the second face's job, which R5 removes |
| Keep the cards and lighten their borders | it leaves V1: the box, not its border, is what repeats |
| Draw the sidebar on the ground plane | the sidebar is frame, not content; on the ground its selection competes with the view's |

### 8.4 Where it is written

`packages/contracts/src/theme/themes.ts` and the adopted sheet `docs/design/system/tokens/colors.css` carry R1 and R2. `packages/ui/src/shell.css` carries R3 to R8 in one block at its foot, headed "The 2026-11-14 revision", after the rules it revises; the sidebar's own rules stand where the rail's stood. No rule there names a literal colour or length.
