# `edits-proof`

What this proof measures and re-verifies. The [proof registry](README.md) carries its area, prerequisites,
platforms, flags and neighbours; the script is [`apps/desktop/scripts/edits-proof.mjs`](../../apps/desktop/scripts/edits-proof.mjs).

Since fin42 (N52): the edit directives PD-TOOLS-014 to PD-TOOLS-018 through the run host on a CRLF, tab-indented fixture the proof generates (`lib/crlf-fixture.mjs`; a committed CRLF file is normalised away), one scripted run of `tool:<key>` steps (`ROTARIS_SCRIPT_TOOL_ARGS`, `@content_hash` filled from the newest hash): a level-1 edit, an insert and a multi-line `replace_all` keeping CRLF and saying so, a level-2 window edit, its no-op refused in PD-TOOLS-016 clause 4's words, an endings-only edit refused in PD-TOOLS-017 clause 3's, a level-3 shift with a `\r`-carrying `old_string`, and a `haet_edit` near miss at level 2 quoting the window with its `\r`s, retried with the quote and applied; asserted from each file's bytes, the stored results and `lib/near-miss-edits.mjs`'s reading. Since fin43 (`PD-TOOLS-019`, OQ-280) a one-tab-shallow LF `old_string` on a CRLF file is asserted at level 3 — `shift-lf` through `write_file` (the shift sentence, then the CRLF sentence, the exact bytes) and `near-lf`/`retry-lf` through `haet_edit` (the near miss naming the line endings, its quote applying); until then it was recorded only, landing at level 4 as given (`docs/defects/fixed/on-a-crlf-file-an-lf-old-string-one-indent-off-skips-match-level-3.md`). Since fin44 (`PD-TOOLS-020`, OQ-295) its `blank-lf` step sends an LF block one tab shallow with an empty interior line onto the CRLF fixture's `blank.js` and asserts level 3, the shift and CRLF sentences and the exact bytes, the blank line kept (`docs/defects/fixed/a-block-with-a-blank-interior-line-never-matches-at-level-3.md`).
