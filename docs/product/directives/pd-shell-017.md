### PD-SHELL-017 — the Workspace is one header band and the transcript, between a Run panel and the inspector

1. **The frame.** The Workspace view holds three columns. The middle column is the work: one header band, then the re-authentication prompt, the plan gate, the transcript, the queued messages and the composer, each when it has something to show and in that order. On its left stands the Run panel of clause 3, on its right the inspector of clause 4 (revision 2; revision 1 put both in one panel on the right). Either may be folded, and the work takes the room.
2. **The header band.** One band heads the left column and replaces the run header, the transcript toolbar's own row and the intent line's own row. It carries, in this order: the run-state label (REQ-CHAT-008), the run-timing indicator (REQ-CHAT-158), the improvement-analysis indicator (REQ-LIBRARY-125), the intent status (REQ-ORCH-014) and the transcript toolbar. The toolbar keeps its controls and their words: "Apply stash (n)", "Clear transcript", the scope toggle (REQ-CHAT-069), "Search transcript", "Terminal". The band begins with the Run panel's toggle, whose accessible name is "Run panel" and which states the number of agents while the panel is folded, and ends with the inspector's, whose accessible name is "Inspector". The band is one line at every width and never scrolls sideways: below a band width of 1040 px the toolbar's text buttons show their icons, a stash that holds something keeps its count, and the intent status and the scope's detail are said to assistive technology and not drawn. With no agent selected, REQ-CHAT-069's reason for an unavailable "This task" is that control's tooltip and accessible description, and is not drawn beside it.
3. **The Run panel.** The left column is a panel named "Run", `--rt-run-panel-width` wide, that scrolls as one. Top to bottom it holds: a head the band's height with the title and "Compress context"; the live run's model selector and its reasoning selector, a line each; the agents with the delegation tree and, under it, a selected agent's "Steer" and "Cancel"; the task plan; and REQ-WORKSPACE-113's project section when it has something to offer. The three live-run controls keep REQ-CHAT-078's enablement rule and its four reason texts.
4. **The inspector.** The right column is the inspector, `--rt-inspector-width` wide. It keeps everything REQ-MONITOR-052 lists, in this arrangement: a head the band's height with its title, "Unpin" while a selection pins it, and a control that folds it; a body that scrolls, holding the mode ("Pinned by selection" or "Following — tracking whichever agent wrote last"), the persona selector and that persona's instances on one line, the agent's dot, name, meta line and scope note, and then four ruled sections, "Context", "Model and reasoning", "Tools" and "Artifacts"; and a foot that does not scroll, holding "Steer", "Pop out" and, at its far end, "Cancel". The "Tools" heading states how many of the agent's tools it has used and one line names them, the one in flight in the running colour; the tool chips, the whole granted set in its own order as REQ-MONITOR-062 keeps it, stand in one disclosure labelled with their count ("All 12 tools"), closed until opened. A persona's single instance that carries the persona's name is not named a third time by the title. With no agent to show the inspector is its mode and the sentence "No agent is selected, and no transcript row names one."; the idle dot, the empty context ring and the three disabled acts are not drawn.
5. **Folding, and what is remembered.** Each panel is shown or folded by its toggle in the band, at every width. From 1180 px the choice is remembered per user with the view's other pane sizes (REQ-SHELL-069): the Workspace stores four panes, the transcript's and the composer's heights and then the two panels' widths, a width of 0 being a folded panel. From 1180 px and below a window width of 1400 px the Run panel stands beside the work and the inspector is the drawer on its side, since both beside it left the work 480 px at 1180; from 1400 px both stand. Below 1180 px each panel is an overlay drawer on its own side, under the band, one open at a time as REQ-MONITOR-051 requires; wherever the inspector is a drawer, choosing an agent opens it; Escape (REQ-SHELL-055), a press on the dimmed work, and the drawer's own close control close it; nothing is remembered about a drawer. The three drawer buttons and their bar are drawn no longer. PD-MONITOR-008's reading of the 1180 px stands.
6. **The transcript and the composer.** Both stand on the ground plane. The transcript has no frame; its rows, and everything else the transcript region holds, keep to a reading measure of `--rt-reading-measure` centred in the column, as do the queued messages and the composer. The composer's card fills the row its divider sizes (REQ-CHAT-010), one interval above it and below it, and the field's text begins on the strip's left edge. REQ-CHAT-070's border treatment while following is one edge, the transcript's lower edge, in the accent and at the reading measure, and is absent while not following; the composer's own border is the same in both states.
7. **What the run waits on the reader for.** While an approval is pending, a line directly above the composer's field names the requesting agent and, in REQ-CHAT-051's trailer words, what it asks to do, with one control, "Review", that opens what the trailer row opens, and a count of the further requests waiting. It answers nothing itself. The trailer row and the approval dialog stand as written.

**Why, and what it costs.** The owner asked for the Workspace to be improved with a free hand over its information architecture, interaction and drawing, functionality preserved ([R-107](../requests.md#r-107)), was shown two concepts drawn in the product's own tokens, and chose this one. Measured on the built application at `131d02cb`, 1440×900, a run with three agents: the transcript column was 800 px of 1440 between two chrome columns of 260 px and 300 px; three bands stood above the first transcript row, four at 1000×680, where the transcript kept 386 px of 680; the live run's model and reasoning could be changed in three places; the inspector listed every tool the agent had not used, a line each; and a pending approval was a transcript row that scrolled out of view. The repository's own operator research ([guide 04](../../research/design/04-the-agent-operator-journeys.md)) says the operator is mostly away and returns, that state must read at a glance with detail on demand, and that what waits on the operator must not be the thing that scrolls away.

What a user can now do that they could not: fold either panel at any width and find it folded the next day; read the transcript at 760 px of 1440 with both shown, 1040 with one and the full column with neither; see the run's agents and its plan together on return; find a pending approval above the field at every scroll position; reach an agent's three acts without scrolling the inspector. What it costs: the live-run controls stand in the Run panel and no longer in the band, so with that panel folded they are one act away; with both panels shown the transcript is narrower than it was before this directive (760 px against 800 at 1440), which is why each folds; the tool chips are one act away, the names of the tools in use are not. The second concept shown, agents as a strip of tabs above the transcript, was not chosen: delegation here goes three levels deep with four children each, and a strip does not hold a tree.

Its case on REQ-MONITOR-043 and REQ-CHAT-078 is where the live-run controls stand: the Run panel, and not the run header; the run-state label and the timing indicator stay in the band. On REQ-MONITOR-052, clause 4. On REQ-CHAT-070, that the border treatment is one edge. [`PD-SHELL-016`](pd-shell-016.md#pd-shell-016)'s revision 5 named the delegation pane "the Workspace's own left column from 1180 px"; it is the Run panel since this directive, and that directive stands in everything else.

Clauses 1, 3, 4 and 5 are rewritten: two panels, each folding and remembered, and the inspector rearranged. REQ-MONITOR-051 was narrowed by revision 1, which had one drawer, and is not since this one: two drawers, one at a time, as it is written. Clause 6 gains the composer's card.
