# Process view — five processes and what crosses each boundary — Message families (continued)

Part 6 of 9 of [the process view](../02-process-view.md), which indexes every part.

**The surveyed shape ([`PD-TRACE-002`](../../../product/directives/pd-trace-002.md#pd-trace-002); REQ-ADOPT-031..052; 2026-11-21).** The board's family gains six members, all on the Part AK path (main replaces `workspace` and stamps the actor; no path and no actor cross, REQ-ADOPT-028) and all answered by the delivery service, which holds the shape host (`workspace-service/shape.ts`): `requirements.shape` (the strip's read: no model, nothing written, no `changed`); `requirements.survey {token?, answer?}` (the survey, or its continuation from the user's words; ends in a preview and carries no `changed`, since nothing is recorded; bounded at 720 s by main's relay); `requirements.shape.accept {token}` and `requirements.shape.record` (each writes the record, reads the board again and carries `changed`); `requirements.shape.export` (writes the repository's shape file; no `changed`); `requirements.criterion {id, index, text, checked}` (one tick, then `changed` naming the requirement). A survey needs a model and the service cannot reach one: per step it asks main on the `ServiceAsk` envelope, **`survey.step {workspaceRoot, messages[]}`** (each message at most 240,000 characters, at most 200 of them), main forwards it to the run host with the canonical root, and the host answers **`survey.stepped {text, model}`** from the `requirements-engineer` role's model, with no tool offered; the service carries out the action the answer names and can only read. Workspace service → main gains one **event**, `requirements.changed {workspaceRoot, generation}`: the service's watcher read the board again, and main publishes the generation on channel 10 to every window, as it does one a command moved. *2026-11-22 ([R-123](../../../product/requests.md#r-123), REQ-ADOPT-026):* the event carries an optional `progress` — one progress-protocol report of a survey in flight (`reading` while the script drafts, then one `adopting` item per step, labelled by its action, out of the step bound), at the generation the board stands at; main publishes it on channel 10's own `progress` field, which `RequirementsChangedSchema` already declared. The board reads nothing again for such a payload; the survey strip folds the reports into the survey's stages. No channel is added (seams §2).
