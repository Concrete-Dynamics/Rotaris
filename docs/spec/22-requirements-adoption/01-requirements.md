> **Decided, not derived** — minted 2026-10-29 at the owner's ask ([R-088](../../product/requests.md#r-088)) under
> [`PD-TRACE-001`](../../product/directives/pd-trace-001.md#pd-trace-001).

## Requirements

### The surface

- **REQ-ADOPT-001** — The requirements board SHALL carry an **adoption strip** beneath its header that states the adoption state of the open workspace in one sentence — *not adopted*, *adopted with no change since*, or *adopted with changes since* — and offers the adoption controls. The strip is not part of the header, and none of its controls SHALL be presented as the primary action: REQ-TRACE-491's one primary control stays the plain re-evaluation control, and every adoption control is a secondary control of the ordinary height.
- **REQ-ADOPT-002** — The strip SHALL offer exactly three adoption controls: **Run adoption** (an initial run, or a re-run where the workspace is already adopted), **Record current state** (shown only where the workspace is adopted), and a **Write layout file** choice that applies to the next run. Each control SHALL describe what it does in wording consistent with it: Run adoption consults a model and writes the adoption record; Record current state consults no model and writes the adoption record; the layout choice writes `rotaris-traceability.json` at the repository root. Beside them the strip MAY offer one **disclosure** control that shows and hides the lists of REQ-ADOPT-023 and the undecided items of REQ-ADOPT-010; it writes nothing and consults nothing. The state sentence SHALL name the new requirements itself, at most five and the rest counted, so that the headline case needs no disclosure.
- **REQ-ADOPT-003** — An adoption run or a recording SHALL start only when the user presses its control. No refresh, re-evaluation, timer, window focus, channel-10 change or opening of the board SHALL start one, and no other surface SHALL start one on the user's behalf.
- **REQ-ADOPT-004** — While a run or a recording is in flight the strip SHALL show that it is, SHALL disable its own controls, and SHALL leave every other board control as it was. Its outcome — the adopted store, the identifier family and the counts written, or the refusal with its reason — SHALL replace the in-flight line when it arrives.

### The detection pass

- **REQ-ADOPT-005** — The detection pass SHALL be **read-only** and SHALL consult no model. It walks the workspace from its root, never follows a symbolic link out of it, and SHALL skip the version-control directory, the workspace scope directory (`.rotaris`), every hidden directory, and the components `node_modules`, `dist`, `build`, `out`, `target`, `vendor`, `coverage` and `fixtures`.
- **REQ-ADOPT-006** — The pass SHALL be **bounded**: at most 20,000 files enumerated, at most 512 KiB read from any one file and at most 64 MiB read in all. Reaching a bound is not an error: the result SHALL say which bound was reached, and SHALL name the consequence among what it could not decide (REQ-ADOPT-010).
- **REQ-ADOPT-007** — The pass SHALL report every **candidate requirements store** it found, each with the directory, the identifier prefix, the definition shape, the number of documents and of distinct identifiers it defines, and the reasons it was taken as a candidate. A definition shape is one of: an identifier block in frontmatter (REQ-TRACE-008), a bold identifier opening a bullet, a heading opening with an identifier, a bold identifier opening a line, and a table row whose first cell is an identifier. An identifier merely mentioned in running text SHALL NOT make a candidate.
- **REQ-ADOPT-008** — For each candidate the pass SHALL state the **numbering** its identifiers follow: `contiguous` where every number from the lowest to the highest is defined, `sparse` with the count of gaps otherwise, `dotted` where any identifier carries a dotted number (`FR-1.2`), and `zero-padded` where any number carries a leading zero (`REQ-001`). A dotted or zero-padded family, and a family whose prefix holds a hyphen (`REQ-AUTH-1`), cannot be written as REQ-TRACE-078's `<PREFIX>-<digits>` under REQ-TRACE-038's `id_prefix`; each SHALL still be reported as a candidate, SHALL be named as undecidable, and SHALL NOT be adopted.
  > **Narrowed by [`PD-TRACE-002`](../../product/directives/pd-trace-002.md#pd-trace-002), 2026-11-21.** A dotted, zero-padded or namespaced family is a shape
  > the survey may propose and the user accept (clause 1); it is no longer refused for its form.
- **REQ-ADOPT-009** — The pass SHALL report the **implementation roots** and **test roots** it found — the top-level directories holding source files, and the directories and file-name patterns holding tests — and the **annotation vocabulary** the code uses: for each source extension, the comment forms in which an identifier of a candidate's family occurs, with their counts and at most five sample lines, each cut to 160 characters.
- **REQ-ADOPT-010** — The pass SHALL report what it could **not decide**, each item by name and with its reason: no candidate store; several candidate stores; several identifier families; dotted numbering; zero-padded numbering; a namespaced prefix; a candidate whose documents mix definition shapes; tests that sit beside the code they test; no annotation of any candidate family in source; a bound reached. It SHALL NOT resolve any of them by guessing.

### The adapter

- **REQ-ADOPT-011** — An adoption run SHALL compose an **adapter** from the detection result and one **interpretation**: the layout document (the keys of REQ-TRACE-038), its annotation conventions (REQ-TRACE-073), and the **extraction** that maps the project's identifier form to the record form — the identifier-block extraction of REQ-TRACE-008 where the candidate's shape is frontmatter, otherwise a definition pattern with exactly one capture group that yields the identifier.
- **REQ-ADOPT-012** — The interpretation SHALL be one bounded request to the model the `requirements-engineer` role resolves to (REQ-CONFIG-098), carrying the detection result and nothing read from the workspace beyond the result's own sample lines. It SHALL be asked to choose one candidate by its identifier, the identifier prefix, the definition pattern, the implementation and test roots, and the annotation conventions. No configured model for the role is a refusal naming the role.
  > **Narrowed by [`PD-TRACE-002`](../../product/directives/pd-trace-002.md#pd-trace-002), 2026-11-21.** The interpretation is a bounded, read-only agent
  > that reads inside the workspace and repairs its proposal for at most three rounds (clause 3).
- **REQ-ADOPT-013** — The interpretation is **untrusted input**. Before anything is written or executed, the answer SHALL be read as one JSON object holding only the keys of the interpretation (Part 02), and every field SHALL be validated: the candidate SHALL be one the detection reported; the prefix SHALL satisfy REQ-TRACE-038's `id_prefix` rule; every path SHALL satisfy REQ-TRACE-043 and name an existing directory inside the root; every convention SHALL satisfy REQ-TRACE-073; the definition pattern SHALL compile, hold exactly one capture group and be at most 300 characters; and the layout document composed from them SHALL parse under REQ-TRACE-045 and REQ-TRACE-046.
- **REQ-ADOPT-014** — The store directory of the composed layout SHALL be the chosen candidate's directory as the **detection** reported it, never a path the interpretation names. The interpretation SHALL NOT be able to name a file to write, and no path it supplies SHALL resolve, after symbolic links, outside the workspace root.
- **REQ-ADOPT-015** — An answer that is empty, carries no JSON object, carries an unknown key, or fails any validation SHALL be a **refusal** naming the field and the reason. A refusal writes nothing of the assistant's, leaves the board and its generation unchanged, and is reported on the strip; it SHALL NOT raise out of the workspace service. The configuration load that resolves the role's model keeps the workspace scope directory ignored (REQ-CONFIG-013) as every load does; that maintenance is the configuration's and not the assistant's.

### Executing and recording

- **REQ-ADOPT-016** — A validated adapter SHALL be **executed** before anything is written: its extraction runs over the store directory and yields, for each document, the content hash of REQ-TRACE-036 and the identifiers it defines, and for each identifier, the canonical hash of REQ-TRACE-037 over its definition text. An execution that yields no identifier of the chosen prefix SHALL be a refusal.
- **REQ-ADOPT-017** — A definition pattern SHALL be applied one line at a time, to lines of at most 2,000 characters; a longer line is passed over and counted. A requirement's definition text runs from its defining line to the line before the next definition or the next heading.
  > **Narrowed by [`PD-TRACE-002`](../../product/directives/pd-trace-002.md#pd-trace-002), 2026-11-21.** A shape's patterns are applied to lines of at
  > most 16,000 characters (REQ-ADOPT-033); this bound stands for the adapter's one-capture pattern.
- **REQ-ADOPT-018** — On a successful execution the run SHALL write the **adoption record** (Part 02) to `.rotaris/requirements-adoption.json` in the workspace, replacing any earlier record whole, and SHALL write nothing else unless the layout choice of REQ-ADOPT-002 was set.
  > **Narrowed by [`PD-TRACE-002`](../../product/directives/pd-trace-002.md#pd-trace-002), 2026-11-21.** Nothing is recorded before the user accepts the
  > preview (clause 2), and Export to repository writes the shape to the repository root on the user's act (clause 9).
- **REQ-ADOPT-019** — With the layout choice set, the run SHALL also write the composed layout document to `rotaris-traceability.json` at the repository root (REQ-TRACE-040) **only where no such file exists**. An existing file SHALL NOT be overwritten: the run reports that it was kept, and the record is written all the same.
- **REQ-ADOPT-020** — **Record current state** SHALL execute the adapter the current record holds, without a model, and write a new record from its result, so that the changes the strip reports are taken as the new baseline. Where no record exists it SHALL be refused.
- **REQ-ADOPT-021** — Every write of the record SHALL replace the file atomically, so that a reader sees the previous record or the new one and never a part of either.

### The comparison on opening

- **REQ-ADOPT-022** — Opening the board, and every re-reading of the adoption state, SHALL **compare** the store as it now stands against the recorded set by executing the recorded adapter. This is a **read**: it SHALL write no file in the workspace or its scope directory, SHALL consult no model (REQ-TRACE-341), and SHALL NOT move the board's generation.
- **REQ-ADOPT-023** — The comparison SHALL report, by identifier and in identifier order, the requirements that are **new** (extracted now and absent from the record), **changed** (present in both with a different canonical hash) and **removed** (in the record and no longer extracted), and the documents added, changed and removed by content hash. New requirements SHALL be reported first.
- **REQ-ADOPT-024** — Where no record exists the comparison SHALL report the workspace as not adopted and SHALL carry the detection result, so the strip can say what an adoption run would start from.
- **REQ-ADOPT-025** — A record that cannot be read, is not valid JSON, carries an unknown version, or whose adapter no longer validates under REQ-ADOPT-013 SHALL be reported as an unreadable record with its reason, and treated as no record for the strip's controls. It SHALL NOT be repaired, rewritten or removed by the comparison.

### Progress and refusal

- **REQ-ADOPT-026** — A run SHALL report its progress through the board's progress protocol (REQ-TRACE-210 to REQ-TRACE-216) and its existing phase words: `reading` for the detection pass and the store extraction, `adopting` for the interpretation, `recording` for the writes.
- **REQ-ADOPT-027** — The interpretation SHALL be bounded at 180 seconds. A model that answers only with reasoning and no content, or exceeds the bound, SHALL be a refusal saying so.
  > **Narrowed by [`PD-TRACE-002`](../../product/directives/pd-trace-002.md#pd-trace-002), 2026-11-21.** The bound is the survey's own, of time and tokens,
  > set when it is built (clause 3); a survey that exceeds it is still a refusal saying so.
- **REQ-ADOPT-028** — The adoption commands SHALL travel inside the board's command family (REQ-TRACE-378): the renderer names the command, main replaces the workspace with the one it has open and stamps the actor (REQ-CONFIG-198), and the workspace service performs every read and write. A renderer SHALL NOT send an actor and SHALL NOT name a path.
- **REQ-ADOPT-029** — A run SHALL NOT alter a requirement document, the generated artifact, a baseline, the retired-identifier log or the delivery store. What it writes is the record of REQ-ADOPT-018 and, by choice, the layout file of REQ-ADOPT-019, and nothing else; the ignore entries REQ-CONFIG-013 keeps are written by the configuration load that resolves the role's model, as on every load, and not by the assistant.
  > **Narrowed by [`PD-TRACE-002`](../../product/directives/pd-trace-002.md#pd-trace-002), 2026-11-21.** A status word and the tick of an acceptance
  > criterion are written into a surveyed document where the shape names their place (clauses 6 to 8), and nothing else is.
- **REQ-ADOPT-030** — Two runs in one workspace SHALL NOT overlap: a run or recording requested while one is in flight SHALL be refused with the reason, and the one in flight continues.

### The surveyed shape

> **Decided, not derived** — minted 2026-11-21 at the owner's ask ([R-116](../../product/requests.md#r-116)) under
> [`PD-TRACE-002`](../../product/directives/pd-trace-002.md#pd-trace-002), as the first act of building it. REQ-ADOPT-031 to REQ-ADOPT-052 are that directive's requirements.

- **REQ-ADOPT-031** — A repository's **shape** SHALL be one JSON object in the format of Part 02 that states, for one to eight **stores**, the directory of its requirement documents and how a requirement is written there, together with the form of an identifier, the implementation and test roots, and whether evidence is owed. An identifier read through a shape is the form that shape declares and SHALL be taken as an opaque string: it opens with a letter, holds letters, digits and single `-`, `.` or `_` separators, and is at most 80 characters. Two such identifiers SHALL be ordered run by run, a run of digits by its value.
- **REQ-ADOPT-032** — A shape SHALL be believed only after every field has been validated: exactly the keys of Part 02 and no other; every directory a repository-relative path that exists and resolves, after symbolic links, inside the workspace; no store's directory inside another's; every pattern a regular expression of 1 to 300 characters that compiles, carries the named groups asked of it and no other, uses no backreference, and quantifies no group that itself holds a quantifier; an `identifier` pattern that does not match the empty string; every status word one line of at most 40 characters that names a lifecycle, a delivery state or both. A failure SHALL be a refusal that names the field and the reason. The same validation SHALL be applied to a shape read from a file as to one a model proposed.
- **REQ-ADOPT-033** — Executing a shape SHALL be read-only and bounded as the detection pass is (REQ-ADOPT-006). A store's `definition` SHALL be applied one line at a time to lines of at most 16,000 characters; a longer line is passed over and counted. A requirement's text runs from its defining line to the line before the next definition, or before the next heading: of any level where the defining line is not a heading, of its own level or higher where it is one. The first definition of an identifier in path order SHALL be the one read and a later one SHALL be counted and reported with its place. A defining line whose captured identifier the shape's own `identifier` pattern does not match SHALL yield no requirement and SHALL be counted.
- **REQ-ADOPT-034** — Where a store declares a status place, the first line of a requirement's own text the status pattern matches SHALL give its status word, with the line and the columns the word occupies; where a store declares acceptance criteria, each task-list item in the requirement's own text SHALL be one **criterion**, with its text, whether it is ticked, and its line. A requirement's canonical hash SHALL be taken over its text **without** the status word and with every criterion's mark read as unticked, so that neither write of REQ-ADOPT-046 changes the specification the board compares (REQ-TRACE-402).
- **REQ-ADOPT-035** — An execution SHALL report, beside the requirements and the documents with their hashes: the identifiers defined a second time; the identifiers a store's documents mention and nothing defines; the documents of a store that define nothing; the defining lines REQ-ADOPT-033 rejected; the lines passed over; and the bound reached, if one was. These are what a repair round (REQ-ADOPT-038) and the drift statement (REQ-ADOPT-052) are made of.

### The survey

- **REQ-ADOPT-036** — The strip SHALL offer one control that **surveys** the repository, and a survey SHALL start only when it is pressed (REQ-ADOPT-003). A survey SHALL first derive, by script and without a model, a **draft** shape from the detection result — candidates grouped into stores by directory, each store's definition the line forms its candidates were found in — and SHALL validate and execute it like any proposal.
- **REQ-ADOPT-037** — The survey's agent SHALL be the model the `requirements-engineer` role resolves to (REQ-CONFIG-098), asked one step at a time, each step answered with one JSON action. The actions that read are `list`, `read` and `grep`; each SHALL be carried out by the workspace service, inside the workspace, never through a symbolic link out of it and never in a hidden directory or one REQ-ADOPT-005 skips. There SHALL be no action that writes. A survey SHALL be bounded: at most 12 reading actions before a proposal is owed, at most 40 steps, at most 8 minutes, and at most 3 consecutive steps that fail or answer nothing.
- **REQ-ADOPT-038** — A proposal SHALL be untrusted input on the terms of REQ-ADOPT-013 to REQ-ADOPT-015: validated under REQ-ADOPT-032, then executed, and what the execution measured (REQ-ADOPT-035) SHALL be returned to the agent so that it can repair the proposal, for at most three rounds after its first. A proposal SHALL NOT set the obligations: a surveyed shape is recorded with `obligations` `optional`. A note longer than its bound SHALL be cut and SHALL NOT cost a round.
- **REQ-ADOPT-039** — A survey SHALL end in a **preview** and SHALL record nothing: the stores with their counts, the identifier form, the number of requirements extracted, samples of them with their document and line, what the board would write into the documents, and what could not be read. The preview SHALL be of the last proposal that extracted a requirement; where the agent gave none, it SHALL be of the draft and SHALL say so with the reason. A survey that ends with neither SHALL be a refusal that names why and leaves the board as it was.
- **REQ-ADOPT-040** — The user MAY answer a preview in words; the survey SHALL then continue from that answer, which is handed to the agent as a correction of the shape and as nothing else, and SHALL end in a new preview or a refusal that leaves the earlier preview held.
- **REQ-ADOPT-041** — Accepting a preview SHALL execute its shape again and write the **shape record** (Part 02) to `.rotaris/requirements-shape.json`, atomically (REQ-ADOPT-021): the shape, each document's and each identifier's hash, and what the shape left unread on that day. **Record current state** SHALL write a new record through the accepted shape without a model. A preview is held in memory only; one that is no longer held SHALL be refused. Two surveys in one workspace SHALL NOT overlap (REQ-ADOPT-030).
- **REQ-ADOPT-042** — **Export to repository** SHALL write the accepted shape, and nothing of the clone's record, to `rotaris-requirements-shape.json` at the repository root. A workspace that holds that file SHALL read its shape from it, validated on every read, and needs no survey; the local record is then that clone's baseline only while it was taken through the same shape. A shape file or record that does not validate SHALL be stated on the board with its reason, SHALL draw no card, and SHALL NOT be repaired, rewritten or removed by a read (REQ-ADOPT-025).

### The board through a shape

- **REQ-ADOPT-043** — A workspace from which the built-in layout reads no requirement, and which has an accepted shape, SHALL be read through that shape: every extracted requirement is a card (REQ-TRACE-242), and its detail view names the document and the lines it was read from, the status word its document states with what the shape reads it as, and its criteria. A workspace the built-in layout reads keeps domain 08 in full. A surveyed requirement SHALL have no editor and none SHALL be created from the board (REQ-TRACE-444): the view says that its text is edited in its own document (REQ-TRACE-435).
- **REQ-ADOPT-044** — A surveyed requirement's evidence SHALL be the places under the shape's roots where code or a test names its identifier, by the shape's `identifier` pattern: a file under a test root, or one named as a test, is a covering test, and any other is an implementation trace. Its lifecycle SHALL be its status word's where the shape maps one and `approved` otherwise; an implementation trace and a covering test are owed only where the shape's `obligations` is `required`, and a verification is never owed, since no check of the toolchain verifies it.
- **REQ-ADOPT-045** — Where a surveyed requirement's document states a status word the shape maps to `backlog`, `needs-update` or `done`, and the requirement rests in another of those three, the pass SHALL record it in the state its document declares, as the system, with the word in the record's reason; a requirement declared `done` is recorded with the text it was declared done against. A requirement that is released, running, under review or blocked SHALL be left as it is. A word SHALL be taken once: the words read are remembered in the workspace scope directory, and a word already taken is not taken again until it changes.

### Write-back

- **REQ-ADOPT-046** — The board SHALL write into a surveyed document only a status word and the mark of one criterion. A write SHALL change that token on its line and nothing else, keeping the file's line endings and every other byte, and SHALL replace the file atomically. In the workspace it SHALL be made only where the document's content hash is the one the board last read; otherwise it SHALL be refused with the reason, and nothing SHALL be merged. A requirement the shape no longer finds, a status that is no longer where it was read, and a criterion whose text at its position has changed SHALL each be a refusal that writes nothing.
- **REQ-ADOPT-047** — When a board action moves a surveyed requirement to a delivery state for which its store's vocabulary has a word, that word SHALL be written in the requirement's status place; where the vocabulary has no word for the state, or the document has no status place, nothing SHALL be written there and the state is kept by the delivery store alone. What was written, or why nothing was, SHALL be reported on the action's result; a refused write SHALL NOT undo the delivery change.
- **REQ-ADOPT-048** — The detail view SHALL let the user tick or untick one criterion, named by its position among the requirement's criteria and by its text. The tick SHALL be written under REQ-ADOPT-046 and the board read again.
- **REQ-ADOPT-049** — A run started from the board for a surveyed requirement with criteria SHALL be given them as what it is to meet, SHALL be told to tick nothing and to change no status, and SHALL be asked to end its final message with a report of each criterion by number as met, with its evidence, or unmet, with the reason. The review surface SHALL show each criterion with what the run reported of it; a criterion the report does not name SHALL be shown as not reported and SHALL NOT be taken as met. Accepting SHALL tick the criteria reported met, and the acceptance's confirmation SHALL say how many it ticks and how many stay unticked.
- **REQ-ADOPT-050** — An acceptance's writes for a run started from the board SHALL be made in that run's worktree and committed there as one commit that names the requirement's identifier; a commit that fails SHALL be said and leaves the writes uncommitted there. Every other write SHALL be made in the working tree and left uncommitted. The product SHALL make no commit on the branch the user has checked out.

### Staying current

- **REQ-ADOPT-051** — The workspace service SHALL watch the store directories, the shape file and the layout file of the workspace it reads, and SHALL read the board again, 400 ms after the last change it saw, when one of them changes by any hand; main SHALL publish the new generation on channel 10 as it does one a command moved (REQ-TRACE-334). The board SHALL also be read again when the window regains focus, at most once in ten seconds, and when a run started from the board ends. Each such read consults no model. Where the watch could not be set or has stopped, the strip SHALL say that the board is not watching, and the re-evaluation control stays the guaranteed route (REQ-TRACE-342, REQ-TRACE-507).
- **REQ-ADOPT-052** — **Drift** SHALL be what fits the accepted shape less than on the day its record was written: more identifiers mentioned and not defined, more documents defining nothing, more second definitions, more rejected defining lines. The strip SHALL state it in one sentence with its counts and the first of its identifiers and files, beside the control that surveys again. Drift SHALL keep every card still read, SHALL start no survey, and SHALL consult no model; a requirement no longer extracted is named as removed by the comparison of REQ-ADOPT-023.
