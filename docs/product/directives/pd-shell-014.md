### PD-SHELL-014 — a persona window and the terminal window have a floor, and what each shows there

The persona window (REQ-SHELL-072) MUST refuse to be resized below 560×420 logical pixels, and the terminal window (REQ-SHELL-081) below 560×360, in either dimension, wherever the display work area can hold that size; REQ-SHELL-167's opening sizes and its aspect rule for a smaller work area are unchanged, and this directive says nothing of a size below the floor that a platform allows despite the request. REQ-SHELL-022 remains the owner of the main window's figures and of what holds at them; the two floors here are cited from this directive.

At its floor, at the renderer's default zoom and in every state the window has, each window MUST meet the four checks REQ-SHELL-022 states for the main window, individually checkable: every control the window offers lies wholly inside the window bounds; every control is reached by Tab traversal; every control's label is rendered in full, the only exceptions being the elisions REQ-SHELL-082 and REQ-SHELL-087 permit; and no control requires horizontal scrolling to reach, with one exception stated here — a tab set holding more tabs than the width shows MAY scroll along its own axis, every tab staying reachable by the Left, Right, Home and End movement of REQ-SHELL-152.

In the persona window, the header of REQ-SHELL-074 and the tab set stay in view at the floor and only the instance page of REQ-SHELL-075 scrolls vertically.

In the terminal window, the tab header of REQ-SHELL-087, the controls row of REQ-SHELL-088 — rendered on one line, in its stated order, with Kill on the far side of the spacer — and the notice slot of REQ-SHELL-089, REQ-SHELL-090 and REQ-SHELL-091 MUST stay in view at the floor; only the output grid gives up rows and columns. These hold while a command runs and after it exits, armed and unarmed, with and without a notice in the slot.

**Why, and what it costs.** The build has declared both floors since 2026-09-12 (`apps/desktop/src/main/main-windows.ts`) and no document named them, so the operator already met a limit the record did not state, and REQ-SHELL-146's "same contract independently of the main sweep" had no size to be checked at: a contract without a floor cannot be measured, and a floor without a contract promises nothing about what stays reachable. The harm sits in the terminal window: Interrupt, End input, Kill and Release control are the controls that stop a command or stop the operator's keystrokes reaching it (REQ-SHELL-088, REQ-SHELL-089), and a window narrow enough to clip, wrap or scroll them away hides the way out at the moment it is needed. The persona window carries the steer surface (REQ-SHELL-073, REQ-SHELL-075 (g)) and is already a scrollable page, so pinning its header and tab set is all it needs. REQ-SHELL-022's "no surface declares a minimum window size of its own" is read as the main window's clause: it sits inside that requirement's single definition of the main window's sizes, and REQ-TOOLS-063, REQ-CHAT-043, REQ-LIBRARY-141 and REQ-SHELL-051 already give dialogs figures of their own beside it. What this costs is the free resize REQ-SHELL-167 promised: an operator tiling several windows on a small display loses the arrangements narrower than 560 px, and the tab set's own-axis scrolling is a reach the main window's four checks do not allow elsewhere. No measurement was taken in this round ([OQ-527](../../archive/architecture/open-questions/71-spikes-and-later-questions-38.md#oq-527)); `debug-ui` takes it at each floor and reports it into the first-executions register, and a red raises the floor rather than thinning the controls. Linux under Wayland may not honour a requested size (the Linux host, R-087) and macOS has no host (IC-3); renderer zoom and OS text enlargement are OQ-495's, so this contract is stated at the renderer's default zoom. A control that cannot meet these checks at the stated floor is answered by raising the floor through a revision of this directive, never by eliding its label beyond the two permitted elisions, hiding it behind an overflow, or moving it.

> **Narrows:** REQ-SHELL-167
> **Extends:** REQ-SHELL-022, REQ-SHELL-146
